GUIDE & DEFINITIONS

Attaché Dealer Heat is a board for dealer-signed options exposure using free delayed quotes. Not advice.

Quick start

  • King — strike with the largest absolute exposure for the active metric (usually GEX).
  • Contested — second strike within ~10% of King abs value.
  • Trinity — SPX / SPY / QQQ agreement on whether spot is above (bearish toward) or below (bullish toward) each King.
  • Heatmap grid — rows = strikes (high on top), columns = expirations (nearest left). Cells = signed $ exposure. Small badges = % vs session baseline.

Metrics

GEX = γ · OI · 100 · S² · 0.01 (calls +, puts −). VEX ≈ ν · OI · 100. DEX = Δ · OI · 100 · S. Charm ≈ BS ∂Δ/∂t · OI · 100 · S.

Expected move = 0.85 × ATM straddle for daily / weekly / monthly / annual expiries. Heatmap strike axis marks D/W/M/A hi/lo bounds; chips above show the ranges.
