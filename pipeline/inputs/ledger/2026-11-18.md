I think it is plainly obvious that we did not get that VIX predicted bounce from Monday, but as I said then when the VIX is above 20 it has a much greater chance of falling into the 15% where the signal fails. We still have yesterdays VIX bounce signal on the clock with tomorrow being the last day, will we get that bounce tomorrow? You'll just have to read on to find out what I think on that. 
SPY closed below the 200DMA for the first time since April. That's probably the biggest red flag out there. It's not really common for the 200DMA to get a close below and get shrugged off and V bottom like nothing happened. Now it is possible to get a bounce after the first close below, usually a particularly violent bounce if it happens, but it typically gets sucked back under within a few sessions.  That's the bad news. The good news is we held that old dark pool level at 729.26 again meaning our last line of defense is fighting to the last dollar. The moving average situation has not changed from yesterday other than the 50DMA closing the distance to the 100DMA to 2.24. As far as dark pools go, we got a new one today added to the chart. Sorry folks. it was 1.1BLN at 732.21 which is above the closing price and to make matters worse below the 200DMA, so they were likely sells. It is also right next to the 732.14 for 1.3BLN that came in yesterday. We just seem to be forming new clusters frequently as of late. 
The oscillators are much the same as yesterday. The MACD giving us another red bar, the Stochastic continuing to cycle down, and the AO giving us another red bar. The RSI is still moving down but it is ever so slightly flattening out. In any case there is still plenty of room to move to the downside. 
 
The MOC stayed fairly consistent at a buy side from first call to the close, only losing the 1BLN level to settle out at 903MLN. 
 
 
The GEX picture is still a nightmare, though it is very lopsided at the moment so anecdotally it is in the right place for a violent bounce. On the numbers though, we'll start with the call wall at 775 which degraded today now down to 526MLN. The put wall is a tie between 725 and 730 (1BLN in 0dte rolling off), and believe it or not 700, with all individually at -1.4BLN GEX. 720 is another minor put wall as it was yesterday. Looking at Friday's monthly expiration, 725 remains the big level across Gamma, Vanna, and Charm. 
 
 
 
 
I am going to do a very brief overview of the cross asset today. We had DXY up, Yields down, gold down (a lot), and oil down again today. So the safe haven trade is looking more and more like a possibility. Only fly in the ointment is once again more of the buying was on the long end rather than the short end of the yield curve. 
 
The VIX printed an inside day today. So we got the vol crush we were looking for yesterday, unfortunately stocks did not get the memo. We are still uncomfortably above 20 so now is not the time to be a hero. On the VIX1D we also got an inside day, and while I was at a glance ready to say this looks like a similar setup to 11/6, unlink then we closed out today just a whisker above the 23.16 line, or as I like to call it the "Oh shit line". This likely puts our speculation about it being like June out to pasture as the last time we got two closes in a row above was March 12-13 which led to a 2 day feeble deadcat bounce on SPY before the next leg down tore through the 200DMA for the first time like. In a way we are in worse shape now because we are already below the 200DMA, however given the VIX1D's aversion to starting above that line more than 2 days in a row we likely see more vol crush tomorrow and get a rally. To find the last time it spent more than 2 sessions in a row above we'd have to go all the way back to the April 2025 Liberation Day fiasco. The VIX3M/VIX spread widened a smidge today, so maybe we see it push to 1.04 tomorrow with a rally. If not we are in more trouble than I thought. 
 
 
 

Overall my take remains the same as yesterday. I think the Anthropic IPO will give us a decent pop to the upside, but I think the institutions that are loaded to the gills with pre-IPO shares are going to be dumping heavily after the initial pop as the de-risking of the AI trade is still in full swing. So any rally has the potential to be short lived. I still think that 725 will be the magnet for Friday unless Gamma/Vanna/Charm dramatically shift tomorrow. In terms of how high we go, the 739.72/738.73 dark pool cluster from 11/16 and 11/17 is the likely peak, with a stretch to 744 to touch the 10DMA. I think we ultimately reject one of those. Depending on how high the bounce goes the 725 by end of Friday could end up being that the 725 level pulls price down to pin at 730. Either way I do not see us spending more than a session or two above the 200DMA if that. The alternative is the Anthropic IPO is such a success that we manage to retake the 10DMA, at which point we may get a much larger and more violent rally that has more staying power, but I would cap that  alternative path at 754. Price action prior to the IPO going live is likely to be fake, and after is likely to be irrational, so if you aren't a seasoned trader, best not to day trade tomorrow.
