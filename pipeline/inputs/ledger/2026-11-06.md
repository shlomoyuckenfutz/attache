I'm not feeling too well today so this one is going to be short, I will give you a longer write-up for Monday. So lets see where we left the week and where we might go. 

Lets start with the weekly chart this time. I think it is abundantly clear that we broke the uptrend. According to the oscillators we still have plenty of room to move lower. Here's what concerns me, the Weekly SMA 10 is looking dangerously close to getting a bear cross with the SMA 20. The last two times that happened were March of this year and March 2025. When they crossed earlier this year it produced the next leg down and we dropped another 6% before finding a bottom, in 2025 however, the cross triggered the next leg down that didn't find a bottom for another 20%. No matter how you slice it, the charts are screaming "but wait, there's more!"
 
The daily doesn't look great either. While it was an inside day, we rejected the dark pool levels and horizontal resistance sending us down to close not far from where we opened. Not exactly a bullish candle. The moving averages continue to realign bearish, the 20DMA is moving away from the 50DMA since getting the death cross, and now the 10DMA is under the 100DMA. Technically we still have not touched the 200DMA, so I do not expect any sort of sustainable bounce until it is actually tapped. I think we will also undercut it for a few sessions before a bounce as we typically do in the opening innings of a nasty correction. The oscillators have more room for us to drop on this time frame as well. We got one new dark pool level today for 1.2BLN at 733.13. So it is clear that this 733 neighborhood had become the new big level, so once the liquidity there is eaten up we will likely see a nasty drop like with 762 ran out. 
 

My take here is first, I am not going to even think about the GEX until open Monday, we all know by now the positioning resets after the weekend, so Friday's closing data is often useless by Monday open. As to where we go, well Monday I do not expect us to move much with CPI due out on Tuesday. My lean is that we drift lower to pin around 733. The alternative is we reclaim some of those dark pool levels above, but I cannot see it getting higher than 740 before CPI. Tomorrow is likely going to be one of those sit on your hands low volume days, so best to just wait until after CPI. I'll have a longer write-up with full details Monday.
