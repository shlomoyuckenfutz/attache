Welcome to the Thunderdome new traders, this is only the tip of the iceberg. Today we found out that not only can SPY decline more than 1% in a session, but it can do that times 4. We certainly seem to be in the aftermath of a bubble pop, and if that indeed ends up being the case them we have a LONG way down to go. Before we get started with where today left us I want to take a moment to point out why this daily write-up is the most important thing for you to read. On Friday I started warning that we are heading for a test of the 200DMA, and well here we are. Granted I did not expect us to reach it in a single day. On Tuesday night I said that the AI bubble would pop with Democrats taking such a wide margin of control in congress because they would seek to regulate ai and data centers causing a slowdown which the bubble cannot tolerate, and today that is exactly why the market imploded. Anyway, lets get into the charts.
SPY came within a whisker of the 200DMA during the cash session today, we actually came down to test it after hours. Death Cross watch is officially over as the 20/50DMAs got it today (I nailed the call for Thursday being the day earlier this week too). The last time we had a death cross was March 13th, though at that time we were a bit further above the 200DMA but still nearby. What happened next we got a dead cat bounce the next two sessions cumulatively totaling around 1.54% and then on day three we took the next leg down making a new low. While this time may be different, it might just be a different flavor of turd. Today's open tested and rejected the 20DMA, and we proceeded to lose the 10DMA, the 100DMA, the EMA 8, the EMA 21. We also closed below the lower bollinger band today.
We lost a lot of support levels today, and while they were all respected when reached, they did not last very long.  We are below every single significant dark pool level except one (this includes the three new ones today), 723.26 stands alone between us and the abyss. Those three new dark pool levels I mentioned, the first was an obvious sell, it was 3.4BLN at 766.43 which was near yesterdays close. The two new ones have the potential to be buys as they came in at the end of the day and the first for 1.2BLN is just a whisker above the closing price, and there was 2.3BLN at 733 the exact closing price. I put a range on the chart as well from 733 to 733.08 as there was significant activity along that entire range into the close. 
The MACD gave us a red bar, the RSI did exactly what it did last Thursday and said "lol nope" and plunged back into a bearish arrangement and is now 3.50 above the oversold level. The AO gave us another red bar. It was the stochastic that was the odd one out today and it stayed dead flat at the exact same value as yesterday (32.11) while the signal line actually ticked UP. 
 
All of the intraday time frames are extremely oversold, but we are going to stick with looking at the 1H. The MACD managed to give us a white bar to close the day, the RSI is very oversold at 24.25, and the stochastic and its signal line are both at the maximum low of 0.00. The AO however kicked out a red bar to close, so there is a possibility of continuation at open before we bounce to pull these oscillators out of oversold. 
 
The MOC today was all over the place. First call of buy side 508 MLN that quickly got at bad as -3.2BLN sell side before recovering into the close for a buy side 948.2MLN final call. 
 
 
The Gamma profile is pretty ugly. Call walls are basically nowhere to be found, and we have put walls ever 5 bucks from 750 to 725 now (except 745). The most alarming addition however is the put wall of 1.1BLN that has built up all the way down at 700. However, we can see the the main put wall is 750 so price may attempt to get back to it before the next leg down. The heatmaps also corroborate this as there was significant bullish activity at 750 for tomorrows expiration at the end of today. 
 
 
 
Looking at the DXY and yields, it is readily apparent this is a safe haven regime now. The DXY remained resilient and caught a bid. Bonds caught a bid too, but when we look at where yields came in most it was the short end. While the long end had a bid, most of the buying was concentrated in the 1 Year. This is screaming recession worries. 
 
 
Gold is catching a bid also, and it is trying very hard to break above 4100. It would seem that we now have an established negative correlation with SPY. 
 
This brings us to the VIX. I said yesterday that it was just event risk coming out and we were not done spiking. Well today we closed +69.70%. Here's the good news, there is now a 95% historical probability of a bounce in SPY within the next 2 sessions. But that does not mean buy SPY calls to play it because the IV crush will outpace the delta gain and you'll be correct and still take a loss. We have good news and bad news for the VIX1D. The good news is that we are in a place where we can see prior spikes have crushed down the following session. The bad news is if this is a systemic issue like March/April then we are looking at much higher first. I would lean more toward the former for now, too many late bears that have faith this is the big one. Regardless, the VIX3M/VIX spread narrowed below 1.0 today meaning we are in trouble and higher volatility is here to stay. The VIX term structure is also in backwardation for the front 4 months, however only the front month is in extreme backwardation. So if we do get some VIX1D crush tomorrow don't count on it lasting.
 
 
 
 

Here's my take, we aren't done selling, this is just the beginning of a large correction, however markets don't move in a straight line and we are very oversold. This was an impulsive move and it was likely overdone by someone having a firesale today. My lean is a bounce likely tomorrow if NFP cooperates that can take us for sure to 740 with a stretch being an attempt to reach that gamma at 750. If we reach 750 I will be reloading puts as we will be taking the next leg down that should tear through the 200DMA. Picking where an absolute bottom would end up is highly speculative so take this with a grain of salt, but I can make the case for as low as 697.84 as a first destination before a bear market rally. The alternative case for tomorrow is we keep selling, in which case 729.26 would be stop number one, and the stretch would be to the June low around 716.
