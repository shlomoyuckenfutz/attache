So without further ado while my sanity is still intact(mostly) let's get on with the short short version. 
Today (Friday) could have been a lot worse all things considered, but we did still end down and rejected both the 100DMA and 20DMA for the 3rd consecutive session. So at this point those two moving averages should be treated as resistance that needs to be overcome in order to unlock higher price levels. The spread between the 20DMA and 50DMA has been consistently widening since the death cross, and is now $5.41 wide. With the 20DMA now below the 100DMA it is going to take a heck of a move up to get them back into a bullish arrangement. We are for the moment holding above the 10DMA, which in my view is not so much a bullish sign as it is a not excessively bearish sign. If we lose the 10DMA then we are looking at a big bear scenario. For now it looks like chop is on the menu, which would be par for the course for a monthly OpEx week anyway. On the oscillators the MACD gave us the bullish touch we were looking for yesterday, and the AO kicked out a second green bar which removed the 11/29-11/30 analog. The RSI continues to weaken toward a bearish touch and both the RSI line and the signal line are below 50 which is not exactly bullish. The Stochastic looks to be running out of gas too and like it want to cycle back down before even reaching the upper extremity. We did get a new dark pool level today for 1BLN at 757.12. 
 
The weekly chart is a bit more bullish looking, but there are still some hints that it may be a trap. Starting with the good news. We closed the weekly candle with a bullish harami, though the wick is slightly above last week's body so its a weak pattern. The stochastic got a bull cross and is trying the cycle back up, the RSI is racing toward a bullish touch, and the MACD gave us a white bar. We also retook the weekly EMA 21. I'm afraid that is where the good news ends. The AO kicked out another red bar. We also rejected the Weekly SMA10 for the fourth week in a row which means it is firm resistance now. We also rejected the Weekly SMA 20 for the third week in a row, which establishes that as resistance as well. To make matters worse those two moving averages are heading for a death cross, and unless we get a major pump this week the will end in a bearish touch most likely. 
 

Once again I am not giving my data driven expectations for Monday because simply it is up in the air again and I do not want to give out incorrect or half-baked information. Sorry. I will say the overarching longer term potential I see developing here is November which we all thought would be extremely ugly will likely end with a whimper not a bang, and it looks more and more each day like December is where things are going to start to get very spicy. We will have to wait and see how Monday goes, and as I said at the start of this, Monday will be the big write-up since I'll have a better idea of things as opposed to this weekend.
