I think the thumbnail picture says it all, if you've seen the movie Goodfellas you know what this is. If you haven't then go watch it now and stop being uncultured swine. I jest, but seriously, doesn't look great out there today. Yields are surging again as is the dollar, Yen intervention is suspected to have been undertaken by the US Treasury, and we get the October PPI tomorrow, which happens to be Friday the 13th, oooOoOoo spooky. Anyway this will be short today since it looks like major participants are waiting for PPI and as such a lot is stuck in neutral, so lets get into today's numbers. 
SPY tried again to retake the 100DMA and 20DMA, but suffered a rejection for the second day in a row. Not good because right now it is technically confirming that the 100DMA has been flipped to resistance. To add to this the 20DMA has now crossed under the 100DMA. Much like yesterday price spend most of the day in no mans land between the dark pool clusters. We did manage to close just below the dark pool level at 757.43. The MACD had a bull touch today, but the RSI and stochastic are starting to roll back over. The AO kicked out a green bar today, but that doesn't necessarily mean a trend change, in fact Thursday 10/29 we got a green bar after a long stretch of red bars, and the next day SPY gapped down. 
 
I'm not going to go into detail about any cross asset that didn't do anything weird today because the market is very much sitting on its hands waiting for that number, so hopefully we'll have a better idea after tomorrow. 
Gamma is still in a transitional phase, so there is no clear read. The net gamma across the board is negative, however the one GEX wall that has > 1BLN is the call wall at 775 (put wall at 757 is all 0dte). A word of caution here though, more than half of that GEX is the first three Friday's of December, with the 3rd (monthly) accounting for just enough to push it over the 1BLN mark. Not saying we won't find that level, but it may not happen until next month. 
 

Gold got crushed today and is now retesting the local bottom. A break below means no support levels until 3699.96. I decline to opine on what it means for SPY because there is a possibility there is a correlation shift again. Confirmation unfortunately requires a wait and see approach.
 
Yields are surging again, as mentioned above, but today it is all coming from the belly of the curve. Pure speculation on my part, but I think this could have to do more with the Yen intervention than US stocks. In any case the 10 Year yield has broken out and is back to the consolidation level from before the most recent leg down. Where I start to get really concerned is the 2 year yield, since it is a proxy for Fed policy expectations. It broke out today. Now the yield is at 4.644% and the highest Fed Funds futures have the rate hikes going by the end of 2027 as of now is 4.75%, so if the 2 year yield gets above that then we have a problem. Right now it is just something to be concerned about as it is uncomfortably close to that level. 
 
 
 
The DXY is testing the 103.23 level ahead of PPI tomorrow. Hot number will cause a breakout.
 
The VIX appears to be finding its footing and primed for another spike. The VIX1D is screaming just that as we closed just a whisker under the 16.08 line, which is the dividing line between VIX expansion (gradual) and VIX spiking (sudden). VIX3M/VIX spread narrowed today, so it would seem that the assessment yesterday that the downtrend is intact was correct.
 
 


My take is conditional based on how PPI affects the market tomorrow. The bear case is we start moving back down toward the 200DMA, and reach it by or before monthly OpEx. Bull case is a clean reclamation of the 100DMA, but even then it only buys us time and a sideways chop, which I see the ceiling being 765. My lean for tomorrow is down, how far entirely depends on PPI which is why I am not going into more detail. I do not want to mislead any of you or give you incorrect info so plain and simple, we need that missing puzzle piece of PPI before we can accurately assess a path forward.
