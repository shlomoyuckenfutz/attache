# Wednesday 10/28 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Well Fed day is behind us now, and all we really were able to glean was that Chair Warsh really enjoys looking out the window. What he sees is really anyone's guess given that inflation is still raging. Unfortunately just when everyone thought we were all clear Google came in with a right hook after the bell with their earnings. So lets dive in to where today left off and then see if anything has changed moving forward.

Despite the Fed SPY gave us another level to level move off the dark pools today. The 769.45 dark pool which was yesterdays closing level was back tested and functioned as today's low. It is painfully obvious given the low volume that the last three sessions have been entirely mechanical moves. We closed right in the middle of the all important double dark pool level at 772.22/772.43, but it was not the high of the day. If we adjust the white trend line to start from the tippy top of 10/6 as opposed to 10/12 where I had it yesterday we can see that we close right under resistance today, and it makes that top wick make more sense. 769.72 came close to being charted as a new dark pool level today but at 933.5MLN it doesn't quite make the cut. We retook the 20DMA today which is curling up now. Meanwhile the 10DMA which is under the 50DMA is also curling up attempting to cross back above. The MACD gave us another white bar today and it is still negative. The Stochastic has reached the centerline, but the way it has pulled away from the signal line is a cause for caution as it means this could be an impulsive move. The RSI on the other hand got a bullish crossover and is above the signal line for the first time since 10/13. Before we get too excited lets go to the AO for the tiebreaker here, and as we see it gave us another red bar deeper into negative territory, so it would appear to be calling BS on this move.

The 1H chart has decided to add to the confusion today, granted the last two sessions where it was ending looking tired and ready to roll over ended up getting busted up. Nevertheless, we can see the dark pool levels were all respected today. Looking at the close the MACD kicked out the first green bar since before lunch yesterday. The stochastic is tangling up in the upper extreme. The RSI continues to gradually push toward the oversold level. However on that closing candle the AO kicked out a red bar, so generally not a good sign.

The MOC today is screaming that institutions rang the register and minted many new retail bagholders today. It is also showing that given we closed between that double dark pool level that there is still a ton of liquidity there which will make it difficult to overcome. The final print of sell side -2.1117 BLN elevated gap risk even before you factor in earnings.

Gamma today was interesting to say the least. This was the first day in a while that we did not have a ton of 0dte gex piled up atm at the close. It did significant damage to the put walls with the only surviving walls being at 765 by a nose, and the main put wall remains at 750. Meanwhile the call wall at 800 broadened and we have the appearance of a new minor call wall at 780.

The heatmaps for Spot gex, charm, and vanna which had heavy emphasis yesterday on 755 for Friday have lifted a bit today, with now 765 showing as the big downside level. It also looks like an attempt was made today to establish 772 as the ceiling going into tomorrow with a heavy amount of selling at that level. Not great.

The 10 Year yield managed to claw its way back above 5% today and it is very clear that the 50DMA is providing strong support. The question that needs to be asked is are we getting ready to turn back up, or is that a bear flag forming on a lower time frame. Too soon to tell, and Fed days usually take a day or two to digest before the real effects are reflected in yields.

The DXY seems to have established the 20DMA as support now, and the candle just barely missed the ability to be classified as a bearish harami. We also clearly have not seen the real move in the dollar yet as the bollinger bands continue to tighten.

Gold is back in positive correlation with SPY, so much so that the daily candles are nearly identical. It tried to make it to the 10DMA but ended up rejecting around the EMA 8. In the new session is it currently heading down along with SPY's AH movement. it would appear that we are in a confirmed downtrend for gold.

SPX stocks above the 50DMA slipped today, not by much, but given the rapid relentless climb it went on this is potentially signalling a pullback here. We need to see that 200dma get crossed back under first before we can be certain.

WTI has reaffirmed the 10DMA as support and continues to grind higher. If those oscillators cool off before some headline can actually ding the price then we are looking at a higher floor being established for oil at $100 which is kind of an end game scenario for the economy as a whole. Fingers crossed for real peace.

The VIX came in a bit more today, but unlike yesterday we did not end near the low of the day and instead threw out a bottom tail. Given the October action we are at the point in which we could call the higher floor which is at 16.50. Breaking this down and by that I mean a close under, would be the signal that the bull times are back. If we hold it then we are likely seeing > 20 very soon. The VIX1D did end the day in the expansion zone after starting the day in the compression zone. Normally I would say we could see some vol crush in the morning as event risk came out because of the Fed meeting passing, but if you look at the hourly time frame you can see that event risk already came out. Looking at the spread on the VIX3M/VIX we managed to widen past 1.12 today which could be an encouraging sign, but before we get too excited we still have a long way to go before breaking the down trend. Something else that caught my eye is mid-late October looks like a mirror image of mid-late July, so the question now is do we get the mirror image of the resolution, which in this case would mean SPY falling off a cliff.

My take today is the plan remains mostly the same as yesterday, the upside case is still 773.76, then a stretch to 777.86. However even with today that is still not my lean. My lean remains the retrace to 765 and then one more test of 762.27 before the end of the month. Given the huge miss that GOOG had with earnings today and the fact that the AH gains that MSFT had evaporated by half from the earnings call (this is why the write-up is late today because I was sitting on the calls), there is a pretty good chance we see 765 tomorrow, or at the very least the 765.40 dark pool level. I would expect the gamma profile to rapidly shift again if that happens which is why I am not trusting the gamma profile today as it would appear to be another one of those flux points. Overall I would say that November at least into monthly OpEx is looking ripe for a correction of anywhere from 5% to 10%, I favor closer to 5% than 10%. Keep in mind, the action we have been getting is on low volume, and the midterms are on Tuesday, so while we mathematically should have started dropping this week it could be a delayed start until a week from today. Earnings from AMZN, INTC, and SNDK are going to be doing a lot of heavy lifting tomorrow. If they go the same way today's went then the correction is a done deal.
Another risk we need to keep in mind is the war is probably starting up again and escalating next week, even more so as Bibi will be trying to cobble together a coalition to form a government, and as caretaker PM he is still in charge and will likely try to use the war entering a new phase as a way to cajole members into siding with him. This means oil will continue to be a persistent headwind for stocks, and the longer it remains up here the higher the chance we get that we will begin to experience demand destruction, at which point we will careen into recession. The economy is perched on a knifes edge right now. Patience is key here, if we confirm this was a bull trap then we likely have the green light to enter positions to capture downside, if not then expect more consolidation chop. Good things take time, that folksy saying also applies to our trading and how we set up. Quick money is gone quick.
