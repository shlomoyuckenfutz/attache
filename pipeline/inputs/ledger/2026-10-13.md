Another waste of a day for this market. It's abundantly clear that no one is really making any moves until after CPI releases tomorrow morning. While 785 is still on the table, there are cracks beginning for form. So before we get into where we go, let's dive in and see where we are as of today.
SPY ended today a whole nine cents lower. Volume was higher than the past two sessions, but it was still very low. We did come down to test the dark pool level at 776.47 as anticipated in the last write up. We almost made it to the EMA 8 with the lower wick, however the 10DMA is still quite a bit below here and it would make sense to test that as support once more before moving higher. The upper wick tried to make it to the 779.10 dark pool level, but the 0dte call wall at 779 acted as resistance rather than a magnet today (Red flag). Currently the 10DMA is in confluence with the dark pool level at 773.76 (still reworking the lines so that's where the double level there is still dotted lines). The 20DMA and 50DMA are now diverging with each other after 7 sessions of a dance running parallel with each other. The 20DMA is now turning up, while the 50DMA is beginning to flatten out. We would really have to nosedive from where we are now to produce a death cross this week, so that is not currently a front and center risk. Keep an eye on it nonetheless. The MACD kicked out another white bar today, the RSI is essentially running sideways parallel to the signal line, and the Stochastic has turned back up into the upper extreme so it's looking like a double dip cycle. I added the Awesome Oscillator for today to show it kicked out a red bar which means that momentum may be slowing down here. I'll post an explanation of the Awesome Oscillator at the bottom of this article. 
 
The 1H chart is similarly indecisive bordering on weakening under the surface. We did close the last 2 hours above the SMA 10 and 20, and the SMA 50 functioned as support (made some nice lunch money with some 0dte calls I bought when it touched this morning). The MACD is attempting to get a bullish cross, while the RSI is attempting to get a bearish cross, and the Stochastic is still cycling up and headed for the upper extreme. The bollinger bands narrowed substantially today and they are essentially lined up with the dark pool levels at 779.10 and 776.47. This is indicative of a large directional move coming, and it is likely that CPI in the morning is the catalyst. 
 
The MOC went nowhere today, with a small buy side finish.
 
 
GEX is still favoring the upside with the 785 call wall being the big level (ignore the 778, that is all 0dte). The put wall is at 772 which is where I would expect a retest before moving higher if CPI comes in not to the markets liking tomorrow. Sounds bullish right? Well when we look at the heat maps for Gamma, Charm, and Vanna we can see that there is an unfortunate trend that has developed. Coming into Monday the big level across all three for Wednesday and Friday was 790, after Monday it dropped to 785, and now after today it has dropped again to 782 (Red flag). 
 
 
 Gold is still sitting on the 10DMA, but the oscillators seem to be running out of gas. Right now it is stuck in between 4240 and 4100, so it needs to break one of them to get a directional move going.
 
WTI is becoming a headwind now with the renewed attacks on tankers and oil infrastructure in the Middle East. Currently above both the 10 and 20 DMAs with the 10 turning up heading for a bullish touch. The oscillators still have plenty of room to run, and we are approaching the critical level of 95.64, above which things start to fall apart for stocks. This could also explain the consistent rolling down of the GEX,VEX, and CEX levels for SPY. 
 
The 10 Year yield got smacked down by NY Fed President Williams saying that he sees one more hike for 2026 and that the hikes were preemptive risk management hikes (mhmmm sure pal). It wasn't enough though because we ended the day with a hammer candle after bottoming just a whisker above the 20DMA. It is still under the 10DMA which is good, but if CPI is in any way hot tomorrow then our pullback in yields will be finished here.
 
A similar story with the DXY, only it ended the day near the lows. The oscillators appear to be rolling over. However we are still above the EMA 8 and 10DMA. It is a bit concerning to me how much more resilient it has been compared to yields. Again, any whiff of hotness from CPI tomorrow and this is going to shoot right back up. 
 
The VIX meanwhile is still riding the 15.20 line. Technically its up today, but I consider this flat with an upward bias. We are eventually going to run out of room between the 15.20 line and the upper resistance of the falling wedge, but again, we likely continue to see the pin until VIXperation next week. 
 
The VIX1D pullback signal we got yesterday worked out again today. The last 5 have been dead on accurate (albeit some days SPY was marginally red, but this signal doesn't predict how deep a next day pullback, just that there will be one). We got another one today with the open below 6.88. We will see how well the signal functions ahead of a major data drop now and whether or not it is less reliable ahead of those days. For now I assume it is a valid signal. 
 
Stocks above their 50DMA ended the day with a Hanging Man above the upper bollinger band, so it looks like we may see some weakness in the coming days. The RSI is not overbought so it's likely not going to be a reversion to the lower bollinger band, though it is not exactly fool proof since this is not a tradable asset. While momentum indicators can still be a guide they should not be treated as gospel for non tradable instruments. 
 

For CPI tomorrow here is what Financial Juice has to expect based on where the release is. Though I would caveat this with saying that given the oil situation I would be less inclined to believe in any rally in stocks from a softer print and would treat it as a selling opportunity. If oil backs off then it's a different story.
 

My take here is that the bull case is still in play, a soft CPI print fake or not could wick us up to 785 easily tomorrow. The million dollar question is whether or not we can sustain that move. The roll down each day in GEX, CEX, and VEX has me a bit concerned that the ceiling this week may be closer to 780. So what I am saying is while we may wick up to 785, I would expect it to be sold and the end around 780. Now if CPI comes in hot, again fake or not, I see us dropping rather quickly to the 10DMA which will be around the dark pool levels at 773.84/773.76. If it can find support there then that is where I see a buy zone for a trip to 780 by Friday, but I need to see it bounce from that levels, not just maintain. With this week's max pain at 770 if we just maintain then we are most likely pinning in the region until OpEx. Either way based on the bollinger bands on the hourly as well as the low volume for the past 3 sessions indicative of institutions sitting and waiting for CPI, I am expecting a large directional move tomorrow. Whether it is up or down depends entirely on what the CPI numbers are. I will say this much, if we fail to break the 10/6 ath this week then it is highly likely that we put in a top and that next week is the start of a nasty correction following VIXperation.
