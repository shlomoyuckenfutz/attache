I am keeping today's write up very brief because we have a jump ball here and I don't want to give incorrect or half-baked assessments when I am not sure with a high enough degree of certainty on where the next move will be. I have an idea of what could happen next, but it's not certain enough to really dive into it. I realistically need to see another day of trading to confirm my suspicions or not. So here's what I do know and can tell you. 
SPY was up today for 2 main reasons, vol crush from event risk coming out, and oil crashing because there are now quantifiable signs that the stance and tone of the administration has shifted and the war may actually be seriously negotiated to an end. So we gapped up and closed +1.65%, that means the selling is over right? Not necessarily. We still closed below the 50DMA and actually this is a rejection of it since we spent a bit of time above it today. The 20 and 50DMAs are now half a buck away from each other, meaning unless we have a ripper or a rally tomorrow, they will get a Death Cross. We got a new dark pool level for 1.9BLN at 754.51. The MACD, RSI, and Stochastic all had bullish movement today, but the AO kicked out a red bar meaning this could be a fakeout. We also rejected the white downtrend resistance line. We did however reclaim the 10DMA and 100DMA, but remember one day does not make a flip to support, we need confirmation and follow-through otherwise we are looking at a false breakout. The extremely long wick appears to mostly be a glitch since it is not corroborated on lower time frames.
 
On the hourly we can see that the white downtrend resistance line is where we stopped and rejected off of. The oscillators are running out of steam but the RSI could still turn back up, and the AO still hasn't kicked out a red bar. 
 
The VIX lost 20.27% today bringing us to just above the higher floor at 16.50. With how much the VIX came in we now have 'hangover risk' which introduces the possibility that this was an impulsive false move, or at the very least we will see mean reversion. 
 
The VIX1D just barely closed in the compression zone, but I would consider this on the line. It is also maintaining its uptrend. So red flag. 
 
The VIX3M/VIX spread, though widened, is still within the current consolidation area, and one could make the argument this is a lower high after a lower low, but again this is why I need to see at least one more day's worth of data to be sure. 
 
I am not including yields, gold, the DXY, or any other assets today because we have a potential jump ball here so they could be throwing off false signals and I don't want to write what turns out to be a wild goose chase. 
I am going to decline to prognosticate on tomorrow except to say that my speculative hunch is that we give some of this rally back. Realistically I can see tomorrow being a nothing day as everyone waits for the next catalyst which is NFP on Friday. I'm sitting in cash now, and frankly that is what anyone should be doing when they are not certain of the next move. If you want to gamble then go to Vegas.
