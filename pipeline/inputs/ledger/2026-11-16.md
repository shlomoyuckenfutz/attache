Today was a giant red flag to me, and not in the bear trap sense, but in the party is over sense. Between the Jensen-gate drama and the fact that it seems like we are being cyber attacked daily, there is just too much for the market to digest and I fear it is about to puke its guts everywhere. It's almost as if all the consequences of the overly bullish hubris of the past few years is catching up with us all at once. This is what happens when a bubble pops and I think this may be it. Now for the new guys, when I say this may be it, that doesn't mean it crashes tomorrow and if it doesn't that I am wrong. This is dominoes falling, and they have been falling for some time, but now we are picking up the pace, and at some point in the very near future if this keeps up we will probably see our first crash wave. First? Yes, if this is going where I think it's going we are staring down the barrel of a multiyear bear market that will have more than one crash wave. Like 2000 this likely will take the "the beatings will continue until everyone gives up" route.  For now lets keep in mind that markets don't go in a straight line, and lets look at where today landed us so that we can triage the situation and maybe sniff out an edge.
Today we began another leg down and though we ended near the lows, we were backstopped by the cluster of dark pool levels at 738.44, 738.81, and 738.93. At the end of the day we also got a new dark pool level for 2.3BLN at 739.72. There were dark pools at 739.71 and 739.70 but they were just a whisker above 950MLN and I don't chart it unless it crosses the 1BLN mark. I mention those however to show there is a considerable amount of liquidity there now that theoretically should help out the 738 dark pools with backstopping price. Remember though, it's a double edged sword, because once liquidity drains out of these big levels they often lead to drops. We also got a new dark pool at 754.17 for 2.5BLN. Below the 738 cluster the next cluster is the 733 to 733.08 range from 11/5 that is cumulatively 7.765BLN and the 1.2BLN 733.13 level from 11/6. Below there all we have is an old level from July at 729.26, and that would be our last line of defense. 
On the moving average front, we lost the 10DMA again today, and if it is not reclaimed by tomorrow then we have a flip to resistance. It's also concerning how steep the gradient on it is. The gradient on the 20DMA has also started to become somewhat steep and is now much too far from the 50DMA to cross back above from one or two ripper sessions. Like it or not, we are now in a downtrend. The 50DMA is now starting to accelerate it's decline toward the 100DMA and they are now only 3 bucks apart.  Considering that spread has been between $10 - $20 for months, this is too close for comfort. The 200DMA is just a whisker under that dark pool cluster around 733 now, which is going to make that confluence a pretty strong magnet. 
Thursday and Friday I said I was worried the RSI was being disingenuous and setting up a bull trap, and today those fears were confirmed as we skipped right past a bearish touch and got yanked into a bear crossover. Same story with the stochastic which has begun to cycle back down. The MACD kicked out a pretty substantial red bar and the AO kicked out a red bar though it was running green most of the day and just barely made it into the red toward EOD. Either way all 4 of the oscillators that I regularly use are universally in a bearish alignment now. 
 
On the 1H chart we can see that SPY tried to hold on to the horizontal support at 746.77 today but we lose it and tore through the horizontal support at 741.26 which I am removing after today since it was not respected. Though you can see how the 738 cluster of dark pools caught the fall and stopped the bleeding. We had a lot of buy volume in the final hour with that substantial spike (likely correlated with the new dark pool). The MACD also kicked out 2 white bars near the end of the day. The RSI is rounding up and trying to come in for a bullish touch, and the Stochastic ended the day with a bullish crossover in the lower extreme. However the AO was consistently red bars all day, so while we may be setting up for a relief bounce, it may be short lived. 
 
The MOC started with a buy side imbalance that was just a smidge under the amount of that dark pool amount, unfortunately institutional traders devoured it like hungry piranhas and we ended with a minor sell side imbalance. Sorry bulls.
 
 
The gamma situation is ugly to put it bluntly.  There are no call levels anywhere near 1BLN now. We do still only have two put walls exceeding -1BLN when looking across all expiries (730 will have -1BLN left once 0DTE rolls off). Net gamma is extremely negative. Since this is a monthly OpEx week I decided to include the strip out for Friday's expiration and it is nothing short of terrifying. There is not a single call wall exceeding 35MLN. Yes, Million. Meanwhile the put walls are large and plentiful with 725 just a whisker under 730, followed by 750 and then 740. These GEX amounts are also very large for a single expiration. Since this is Monthly OpEx week we also need to consider the big levels on Vanna and Charm. If we look at the heatmaps we can see the standout level for Friday across both is 725. When we combine that with the large put GEX level at 725 for Friday, we can begin to see that may be our destination this week.
 
 
 
 
WTI came in today by 4.34% today getting us back below the 87 line. Now a few months ago this would have been great for stocks, so you would be forgiven for assuming that now. Unfortunately when we look at the rest of ther cross asset analysis in this write-up we will see that it is potentially not a welcome sight. 
 
Gold continues to sell off. It lost the support of the prior bottom at 3875 today and as of writing is down further in the new session. To make matters worse the oscillators are suggesting that there is plenty of room for more selling. But wait, there's more! All of the moving averages, ALL OF THEM, are now in a bearish arrangement which will make re-establishing a bullish trend for the shiny stuff very difficult. Should the selling continue we do not run into a major support level until 3699.96.
 
The DXY has fully broken out of the 103.23 resistance level and we don't have another resistance level until 104.66. The oscillators are also signalling that there is still room to move to the upside. I cannot stress enough that the DXY is now in a place where it will start hurting megacap earnings, so we are in a very uncomfortable place for stocks. 
 
That brings us to the yield on the 10 Year. Why do I always focus on the 10 year and only talk about the 2 year when Fed Funds is in focus? The 10 Year is a referendum on the state of the economy. That being said, it would appear that we have a reversal playing out on the 10 year yield, and it came in just a bit after Friday kicked out what look like a topping doji. The yield is still well above 5% after last week's abysmal inflation numbers. So we would think that yields reversing and heading back down would be good for stocks right? Not necessarily. If we look at where in the maturity curve the buying was coming from today we see they were buying the belly heavily, buying the short end lightly, but selling the long end. I think you see where I am going with this. If you don't I'll explain in the next paragraph.
 
 
When we put together Gold selling off, oil cratering, and a DXY that rises while yields fall (remember they have been moving together so this is a shift), as well as where on the maturity curve is being bought and where is being sold, the picture that is painted is a textbook flight to safety trade. Now one day does not make a trend, but if this continues it means the big guys are past the point of just buying protection and they are heading for the hills because they think the bottom is about to drop out. 
Which brings us to our old friend, the VIX. Possibly the one ray of sunshine for the bulls. We ended the day +18.71%, and that means there is now an 85% probability of a bounce on SPY within one of the next two trading sessions. Let's not act like this is ideal though as the VIX is above 20 now, and this is where you more often than not find that 15% where the signal fails. The VIX1D ended the day above the 16.08 normal ceiling line. Normally this would mean we are going to see the VIX eat it big time tomorrow. Unfortunately within the last month we have seen closes above that line 9 times, with recent action having the heaviest concentration. Normally you see one or two closes above then we are back into the normal range between 6.88 and 16.08, including regimes where the VIX gradually but consistently rises. When the VIX1D can't get enough of spending time above 16.08 and remains resilient it means something is wrong and something big broke. The last time we saw this was June when we got that nasty pullback. However when you factor in that when it does come back into the range lately that it barely cracks under 12.35 into the compression range, it starts looking a lot more like late February than early June. The tell will be when it starts treating 16.08 as support, that's when SPY starts dropping with gusto.
On the VIX3M/VIX spread we saw significant narrowing that confirms the downtrend is still intact. If we take out the prior low at 0.91 then we have a major problem. Today we closed at 1.01. Either way this spread is signally higher on the VIX now. Lastly, the futures term structure has the front month in backwardation again with spot above. This is the beginning of a problem and as long as this contract is backwardated SPY will continue to face selling pressure. If we get to the point where multiple months are in backwardation like March, then we are looking at a steep drop on SPY. With  those November contracts slated to expire on Wednesday this week we need to keep a close eye on the December contract. If it starts spiking on Wednesday then we are in for a much longer downtrend on SPY. 
 
 
 
 

My take today aside from buckle up is that we are looking at the next leg down that will bring of to kiss the 200DMA for real this time. I fully expect to see 730 by the end of this week if NVDA's earnings call tomorrow does not quell ALL of the uncertainty surrounding Jensen's departure. If they can't pull that off then we are looking at the bubble popping in real time. For tomorrow however, my lean is we get a relief bounce, between how the hourly chart looks and how major market participants will be sitting on pins and needles waiting for NVDA earnings volume should be thin tomorrow allowing us to bounce. At a minimum I would expect us to reach 746.77 and possibly overshoot slightly to test the 10DMA. If for some reason NVDA earnings are okay but still leave a lot of questions unanswered then I see event risk coming out of the VIX1D allowing for a further rally on the back of vol crush that will take us to that 754.17 dark pool that came over today. Ultimately I think that reverses Thursday. If NVDA does not pull off a stick save then we are tearing through that 200DMA like it's not even there and heading for 725. The alternative case is with this being monthly OpEx week we get chop between 733 and 750 that delays the breakdown another week. It's not my base case, but I also don't see it as highly unlikely given all the balls in the air right now and all the data starting to point to December being the ugly month. We are in what I think are the opening innings of a bear market, this is not the time to be a know-it-all if you're new to this. This is the time to defer to the old hands who have been through this type of thing before. I promise you that no matter how many data services you pay for and how many discords you're in, if you haven't traded a real bear market before then you are not ready for what is coming. Don't be a hero here because it's not even gambling anymore where buying the dip gives you a chance at big money, this is more like the casino has rigged the slot machine and no matter how much money you dump into it you will not hit the jackpot. Don't bet against the house here, bet with it. That means be smart, tighten your risk management, and say a prayer to whatever god you believe in.
