# Friday 10/9 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Despite the chaos SPY continues to march higher. The iron grip of 0dte options continues to tighten, though we are nearing the point in which that grip just outright breaks everything and the cracks under the surface within the macro outlook are the harbinger telling us just that. But we can talk about that in another article, for now lets take a look at the current snapshot in time and then figure out what the next move might be. 
Pretty good day for SPY, we finally got a nice wide body candle that isn't a doji, but volume was nonexistent. This may sound crazy, but this weeks gain of 1.16% was entirely just Monday and Friday's price action. The moving average picture is the same as Wednesday and Thursday, and the 50DMA keeps creeping ever closer to the 20DMA, now only 0.41 apart. We got a new dark pool level at 773.84 for 1.1BLN, which may not sound like much, but when you combine it with Thursday's dark pool level of 773.76 then suddenly we are seeing the makings of a new major level. (Side note, I am going to revamp the chart levels a bit on Monday, there is still too much clutter, so major levels formed by groupings will likely be combined and done with something other than the dotted line.). The oscillators appear to be producing a counter trend rather than a stick save, with the MACD giving us a green bar and the RSI moving a bit higher, but on the RSI the signal line is staying put.  Additionally the stochastic continues to cycle back down. We seem to have hit resistance at the 779.10 dark pool level we added on Wednesday. 
 
The Weekly chart is a similar story, MACD got a white bar and the RSI is rising a bit while the signal line stays put, only difference is the stochastic is cycling up here. With how little room the RSI has left and the stochastic at a prior rejection level its likely this upcoming week will form a top if this is indeed a counter trend. 
 
The MOC was a nothingburger today.

GEX, Charm, and Vanna going into this monthly OpEx week still overwhelmingly have 790 as the bit level. We will see how it adjusts on the open Monday accounting for the Friday options settlements, but as of right now it seems to have pulled 790 forward to Monday as opposed to it being Wednesday on 10/8. There still is a significant build up there for Wednesday, just nowhere near what is currently showing for Monday. The only fly in the ointment (and it's a pretty big one) is that GEX has it's largest levels for Monday at 778 and 780, effectively creating a pin level at 779.
 
The 10 Year yield backed off for a second consecutive close below the 10DMA. All the oscillators continue to roll over. Additionally when we look at the weekly candle, it looks like a topping candle. So it would appear, at least until CPI this week, that yields will continue to back off for now. 
 
Gold had a fantastic day. We finally got a close above the 10DMA, but now we need to see follow through. It would appear that we are in fact heading for a test of 4240. 
 
It's not all sunshine and rainbows though, WTI continues to be stubbornly holding on to 91.08, and with the amount of tankers and refineries hit this weekend I can imagine this will help to keep the 10DMA as support. This could hurt the bond rally if it keeps up. 
 
Then we have the DXY which despite yields relaxing, continues to have upward pressure. The EMA 8 is a solid support now. If we can't get a meaningful pullback on the DXY off of CPI this week then it will derail the bond rally.  
 
Then we have the VIX, which is back in the beachball zone under 15.20. The question is do we get an extended stay here because VIXperation occurs the Wednesday after monthly OpEx this month, or is the VIX1D wagging it's finger saying not so fast. And well... that's exactly what it did, we touched 6.88 today which triggers the next day pullback warning. Then you have the VIX3M/VIX spread which is continuing to widen, but still very much in a sideways chop pattern. So conflicting signals abound. My two cents, this is just the spring still coiling. 
 
 
 
Stocks above the 50DMA is in a legitimate breakout now, well above the 10 and 20DMA measuring this metric. Although it is getting a bit long in the tooth already, with momentum appearing to deteriorate on the stochastic. I wouldn't compare this to April as back then by this point we had already had a massive gap up which helped that rally become the lockout rally it was. We don't have that this time. 
 
 
The big open interest level for Monthly OpEx on Friday still is sitting at 750, and Max Pain is still 768.
 
 

Here's my take, there is a good possibility we get a pullback Monday. Some of you may have already seen my expected path, but it is being revised now. I see a pullback to 773.84 where we bottom, turn, then head up to 785 by some point on Wednesday (CPI is the likely catalyst), after that on Thursday and Friday we head lower and end with a down week that sets us up for a legitimate correction the following week. I would be very surprised if we make it to 790 but it's not out of the question. Likewise I would be very surprised if we got under 772 this week. So my base case is a chop higher. The only possible alternative I see here as of right now is CPI comes in red hot even with the cooked up numbers, at which point October rate hike expectations soar, then we likely will not see much upside this week, but even there we have until Wednesday. Only the bond market is closed on Monday, but I don't think that prevents the pullback given the anemic level of volume already so I don't think the lack of volume will make much of a difference. I do think the bond market being closed will help to limit the amount of downside in said pullback which will allow us to quickly turn back up going into Tuesday. We always these cases are subject to change with changes to the data, but I think we are playing the Icarus game here, seeing how high we can climb before our wings melt, at which point it will be too late.
