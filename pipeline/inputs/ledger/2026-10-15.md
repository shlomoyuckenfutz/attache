# Thursday 10/15 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

It's monthly OpEx eve. Today PPI came in red hot and initially SPY dropped but managed to recover soon after open. Overall not exactly the type of V bottom action you see when we are about to rip. This looked like a cautious probing of the major dark pool levels. Before I get too stuck into my prognostications lets see where today left us.
We had slightly lower volume than yesterday. Despite opening below the 50DMA we reclaimed it and managed to close just above the 20DMA. The 20DMA is going to be the decider here, if we manage to close below it tomorrow then it likely means today was a dead cat bounce, if we get a close above then we have a potential false breakdown yesterday. Now the 20 and 50DMAs have begun to diverge again, but the 50 has flattened out, so if we spend another few days at or under the 20DMA then we will have a death cross. The low of the day almost made it to the dark pool at 764.19 but got close enough for buying pressure from there to come in. But we rejected near the end of the day off of the double dark pool level at 772.22/772.43. The reason I stress this double level so much is that it has functionally been the ceiling since August 4th. While we occasionally spent a few session above, ultimately price was always pulled down underneath it again, and it was really only twice we tried to establish above, August 12th - 14th, and last week into early this week. Every other attempt in between has been met with immediate rejection that sent price back down for multiple sessions. I've changed the color of this level on the chart to cyan so you can more easily see what I am talking about. The oscillators meanwhile don't look great, the MACD is now in a bear crossover by a whisker, and the histogram has a red bar. The RSI ticked up but the signal line is still curving down ever so slightly. The stochastic meanwhile is cycling down much harder. To clear up any confusion from the RSI I'll include the AO as a tiebreaker today, and as we can see it got a red bar.
 
As much as I was hoping that I could clean up some dark pool levels on the chart for not being respected based on the wide daily candle, when we look at the 1H chart it is clear they are all still being respected, whether acting as support, rejection, or consolidation levels, price likes to touch them all and hang out around them. This is one reason I prefer using dark pool levels over fibs, since they are the sites of large trades they are more likely to be sought out rather than formulaic drawn lines on the chart. Also we can clearly see both the rejection of the 772.22/772.43 level, but we can also see the dark pool level at 769.45 is an important level as price has either paused at or run along it for the past two days. The oscillators are a mixed bag, the MACD got a bullish crossover near the end of the day, meanwhile the Stochastic is running out of steam in a cycle up, and the RSI rejected off of the 50 level and appears to be heading back down toward the signal line which is still sloping down. 
 
The MOC today was a sell side that started at -2.3BLN and ended close to even, but that is to be expected the day before a major expiration.
 
 
We won't touch on GEX for all expiries today given tomorrow is a major expiration. But wen we look at the landscape for tomorrow we can clearly see that 772 is by far the largest level at the put wall. Meanwhile, Max Pain is at 770, and when we look at total open interest for tomorrows monthly expiration the largest level is the puts at 750, however locally it is 772. When gauging the open interest pin however I try to find a level that is big and has a mix of puts and calls as close to even as possible, which tells me that would be an equilibrium level. For that we have 770.
 
 
 
The VIX is still broken out above the wedge despite crushing a bit this afternoon. Today counted as the VIX predicted bounce in SPY since we ended the day in the green so that is no longer a thing tomorrow. Again, I am not expecting the VIX to really start to spike until after VIXperation next week. The VIX1D is maintaining strength and we got a second close in the expansion box, and because we haven't pierced 16.08 there is no immediate risk of a reflex vol crush. If anything it wouldn't surprise me if we see volatility rise into the close tomorrow with all the bellicose language and sabre rattling the president has been doing this week. The spread on the VIX3M/VIX hardly moved today, if this was truly a sustained bounce with vol crush on the horizon then we would have see the spread widen more than a single basis point. 
 
 
 
WTI oil had a strong day and it broke out of the resistance at 95.64. Right now it is at the level where it becomes an immediate headwind for stocks. As of writing the next day's session has started and though it's come in a bit, it is still above the 95.64 level. If Iran hits any tankers or the Houthis hit any Saudi oil infrastructure overnight then we will see oil push higher. I think this has legs to 99.10 where it will close that gap.  
 
Here's where things get tricky, Gold, yields, and the DXY are behaving oddly. There are two possibilities, one being this is a divergence event, the other being we have a flight to safety in beginning. I have no lean yet because I need to see more first. 
Let's start with Gold which was the most well behaved of the three, it closed above the 10DMA today but realistically it went nowhere during the New York session today as most of the upward movement was overnight. Nonetheless it still was predictive for where SPY would go today with the positive correlation. 
 
The 10 Year fell off a cliff today, and that cliff was the 20DMA. That tail on the bottom tells me that we may have seen the capitulation move on this pullback and it may be almost ready to move back up or at least consolidate. Here's why it's misbehaving today, forget about the fact that oil is raging, the fact that PPI came in as hot as it did and yields FELL is not supposed to happen. Yields should have spiked with that PPI, and yields are not like stocks where they can just meme it into an irrational movement. There was no other catalyst today. So if yields continue to dump, then what I see is a market that is jittery about the AI slowdown on top of the fact that it is now jittery over stagflation. That means it is possibly a flight to safety.
 
But then you have the DXY. Again, PPI coming in as hot as it did should have spiked the DXY, instead it lost the 10DMA. Here's where it gets even more odd, if this was a flight to safety then the DXY would be up on safe haven demand. So for the moment I really do not know what to make of this and I need to see more before I can formulate a hypothesis. 
 

Here's my take, we have a major monthly expiration tomorrow, and most of the gamma that made for the weird movements of the past few weeks will be gone after the close so we'll have a clearer picture of next week hopefully. For now I see 770 as the pin for tomorrow with bookends of 768 and 772. So we may chop within that range. Yes the range is narrow, but how we close tomorrow within it matters a lot. A close under 771 puts us back under the 20DMA which would be a bearish sign for next week, whereas above 771 puts us on more solid footing, though realistically those 772 dark pools need to be recaptured, they are the key to all this and effectively the ceiling for this market where price does not survive long above. As usual with monthly OpEx we should expect plenty of shenanigans tomorrow, so probably best to sit this one out if you trade 0dte as you can very easily get chopped up and juked if you are not nimble and experienced. But again I expect 770 to be our pin for tomorrow, so if you like condors then as long as we aren't having a trend day then bon chance mon ami.
