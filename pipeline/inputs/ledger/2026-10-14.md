CPI came in hot today and we finally got a down move that was more than 1%. Does this mean we crash, no. Does this mean we have problems now, yes. Mainly if the CPI they are reporting is this hot, then that means the actual figures are far worse. But we aren't here to talk about the macro, so lets dive into the charts and see what the next move may be.
Today sliced through a lot of dark pool levels, some bigger than others, such as the zones from 772.22 - 772.43 and 773.76 - 773.84. We also got a new dark pool level to add to our charts at 777.86 for 1.4BLN. On the moving average front we lost the 10DMA, 20DMA, EMA 8, and EMA 21. For a while it also was looking like we might lose the 50DMA, but we managed to find support at the double dark pool level at 767.41/767.42. Remember, one day of loss of an MA does not mean it is confirmed, you need confirmation and follow through, until then it could end up being a false breakdown which would be a bullish slingshot. Always wait for confirmation. The oscillators are tipping over now, the MACD has a bearish touch and both the RSI and Stochastic are rolling over and cycling down. Volume today was much higher, though still pretty low compared to the average for the last few years.
 
The 1H chart is telling us that we may have overdid it to the downside today. We reached the SMA 200 just as things were getting very oversold AND outside the lower bollinger band. The 200 provided support and we have since began a bounce in which according to the oscillators still has plenty of legs. 
 
The MOC started out with a decent buy side imbalance, but that got eaten up, for a time it was looking pretty bad to the sell side, but the final call relaxed a bit leaving us with a small 581.6MLN sell side.
 
 
The gamma situation has deteriorated significantly today, the put wall at 769 is now the largest net level across all expiries (770 is mostly 0dte). Call gamma for Friday's expiration is gone and the but put level is 772. This is all lining up with max pain for the monthly expiration which is at 770.
 
 
 
Gold looks like it is trying to confirm 4100 as solid support, despite closing under the 10DMA it made a similar move to 10/7. We really need 2 consecutive closed under 4100 to consider the level lost, but we have yet to get one so for now we are hanging in there.
 
WTI came up to test the 95.64 level today, remember above is when oil really started to become an immediate headwind. The bollinger bands are also narrowing which signals that a large directional move is incoming, lets hope to the downside.
 
Here's where things get a little interesting. Despite both the DXY and the 10 Year yield ripping off the back of CPI, they both actually relaxed a bit over the course of the day. The DXY is maintaining the prior high despite the attempted breakout. Meanwhile not only did the 10 Year yield end a bit lower, it also appears that the front end of the curve was getting sold to buy the long end. Flight to safety? Too soon to tell, but it is something we need to keep an eye on. 
 
 
The VIX may have gone a bit too far too soon today. While we did close above the wedge resistance, again a single day does not make a breakout. We got a close above the wedge on 07/29 and it dumped right back in the next day. Additionally the VIX closed up more than +10% today which means there is now an 80% probability that SPY bounces within the next two trading sessions. The VIX1D meanwhile did not trigger another next day pullback signal as we didn't touch 6.88. We are in the expansion zone, but I do expect a degree of vol crush tomorrow as the PPI event risk comes out of the pricing. Also, even though the spread narrowed on the VIX3M/VIX, it is still well within this sideways grind structure, so it could either be a failure to confirm or that a volatility move is starting but has not yet gotten momentum yet.
 
 
 
As expected Stocks Above the 50DMA eased off today, but not as much as tech got whacked. It is possible we see a rotation out of tech before any real correction starts. 
 


Before I give my take I am going to preface it with this, I am leaving out any price forecasting beyond Friday until we get through Friday. There is a lot up in the air right now and we need to see how OpEx settles out before moving beyond it. So here's what I see for the balance of the week. My base case is a bounce here that takes us to the 773.76/773.84 dark pool levels, with the reach being the new dark pool at 777.86 as the past few weeks when these way far away levels came in price usually found its way there within two or three sessions, so while I think it's unlikely I am not ruling it out. Now if PPI comes in hot tomorrow then there is no question about it, we are in a breakdown and this will take us down to the massive dark pool level at 762.27. That's basically the last line of defense give the size of the level, so if that breaks we will be moving rather quickly to the downside. I don't think this will be the case though and my lean is in the bounce case. I know I said that I won't get into next week just yet, but I will say this much, my lean with the bounce case is not a call for a new ath, rather I see this bounce as failing and sending us back down to kick off a correction, I just think the max pain pin at 770 will keep a lid on any violent moves until after OpEx. Be patient when positioning because this is the place that the big guys like to screw you in both directions.
