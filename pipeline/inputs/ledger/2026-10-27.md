# Tuesday 10/27 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Happy Fed Day Eve everyone. SPY had another decent day, but we are running into the FOMC which could be a cause for some concern. All too often we see Fed meetings act as turning points for SPY and with use running up into resistance the day before I hope some of you took some chips off the table just in case. So lets dive into the charts and see where we are ahead of this all important Fed meeting.

SPY managed to recapture the 50DMA on a low volume day, I say those two together in the opening sentence to make this point, don't trust it. I'm not saying that alone  means it was a fakeout, but the fact that we rejected the re-test of the 20DMA today isn't boding well. In addition to recapturing the 50, we also retook the 10DMA, unfortunately that is because it had a bearish cross with the 50DMA today and is now underneath it. We managed to close just above the 769.45 dark pool level, and we tested the 765.40 dark pool level as support today, which by the way was where we closed yesterday. We also opened at the 767.41 dark pool which happens to be where yesterdays high rejected. These last two are potentially encouraging signs. There is a new player on the board (errr chart) today, there is now a trend resistance line that can be drawn from where the downtrend started on 10/12 and we came within a whisker of it, so statistically it is likely qualified as a rejection. The good news is as long as the Fed or Earnings tomorrow don't throw a wrench in the gears, we now have a defined trend, finally. The oscillators are typical of what we have been seeing at inflection points. The MACD is firming up with another white bar, but it is still in negative territory. The Stochastic got a bull crossover in what looks like it could be a start to cycling back up, but be aware that now it is out of the lower extreme that it is in a position where it can allow another drop. The RSI gave us a bullish touch today, this is our first one since 10/21 where it rejected, will this time be different? The AO seems to think so as we got another red bar in the negative territory so it seems to be calling BS.

The 1H chart has once again ended the day in a place where it looks like it wants to roll over with the oscillators screaming at us "we're tired boss", so we need to ask if the market is going to tell it for a third day in a row to keep on chugging.

I'm including the 30m chart today because it put on a clinic as to why dark pool levels matter. We had not one, not two, but three level to level moves today intraday, and after the third that dark pool functioned as a support consolidation level very clearly for the rest of the day. Again, this is why I hardly ever use fibs and prefer dark pools, as it represents actual institutional money at risk.

The MOC wasn't great for the bulls today. While it did produce a smaller final sell side imbalance, if you were watching it live you would see in between it flipped to a buy side and then slipped right back down to where it ended at sell side -509.5MLN, so not a great sign of institutional appetite.

The gamma profile is much the same as it was yesterday and still negative (it will reflect that once the 4.4BLN in 0dte for 770 rolls off at open). The put wall remains at 750 and the only change is where was a smidge more put into the 800 level allowing that call wall to just barely cross the 1BLN threshold.

Taking a look at the heatmaps for the balance of the week for spot Gamma, Vanna, and Charm we can see that we may have a problem. There is a considerable level built up at 755 for Friday which given the price action shouldn't really be there. It was there yesterday but grew today. I am including the GEX volume and open interest heatmaps today just to highlight how active that level has been. So there is a chance we could be heading back down after the Fed.

WTI oil managed to close just a whisker above 100 (currently just below in the new session). No matter how you slice it, this is a major headwind for stocks and the economy as a whole. The outcome of the Israeli election will play a heavy role in what happens next with oil. I can see oil coming in a bit more if Bibi can't solidify a government coalition behind him or another party gets enough to take PM, however if he can we will likely see oil move much higher as the base assumption will be US ground invasion of Iran after the midterms.

Gold looks to be making a move to re-test either the 10DMA or 4100. Unless we get a very dovish Fed tomorrow I don't see either one succeeding. Even from the daily time frame I can easily eyeball that it is in an hourly bear flag.

SPX Stocks Above their 50DMA has completely stalled out since Thursday. I would like to  imagine this is due to everyone waiting for the Fed, but the reality is more likely it's tired and may have rebounded a bit too far too fast. Question that I cannot answer yet is, are we just consolidating, or is this a reversal point.

The 10 Year yield is having a bit of trouble getting back above 5%, as it seems to be acting as resistance now. Though keep in mind this will change rather easily depending on what the Fed says/does. For now it is potentially good news.

Unfortunately the DXY continues to rage higher. I find this especially concerning as it should have been muted with an FOMC decision due tomorrow. Either someone knows something and it front running, or we are setting up for Warsh to murder the dollar with a dovish Fed (doubtful). On the technicals it looks pretty bullish now, and the pullback to cool it off ended up being pretty shallow and more of a pullback in time than in price. A crack above 103.23 would be less than ideal as above it doesn't run into the next resistance level until 104.66.

Finally we come to the VIX. Boy oh boy this is not the print you want to see the day before a Fed meeting. Closing down nearly 5% with a VIX1D rising nearly 20% to just peek into the expansion zone tells me that the market is not taking this meeting seriously enough. We've seen this time and time again, and it almost always ends the same way, short volatility traders get pants'd. This is further reinforced by the fact that the VIX3M/VIX spread can't manage to widen more than 1.12. Remember, when this turns down, the VIX turns up.

Tomorrow we have the FOMC, which I expect to hold rates with a hawkish tilt. Given where oil is I cannot see any reason for the Fed to reel in any of the hawkishness from last month's statement. I'll get deeper into this with a post tomorrow prior to the 2PM decision. I will post here the cheat sheet I got from Financial Juice which shows how different assets should behave in reaction to the Fed under the different outcomes.

We also get MSFT, GOOG, and FDX earnings after the bell, and the following day will be INTC, AMZN, and SNDK. So clearly the next two days will be make or break for SPY.

My take is I am expecting we jog in place until the Fed, but ultimately my lean is we end the week at or below 760. There is just too much activity at that 755 level for just Friday to ignore. Running into trend resistance doesn't help either. The only bull case I can see, and this is not my current lean is that either the Fed is dovish, or the Fed is in line and earnings knock it out of the park with no doubts about ai capex, that would break us out of the downtrend line and I see such a move going to 773.84 first and then depending on how much gas is left in the tank 777.86 aided by the pull from the calls at 780. Even in that scenario I do not think we see a new ath. Patience is the name of the game for the next few days, and remember, the first reaction to the Fed is usually the trap move, don't get suckered in.
