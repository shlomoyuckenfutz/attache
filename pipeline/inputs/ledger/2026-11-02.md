# Monday 11/2 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Houston, we may have a problem. It would appear we are starting the post-midterm correction early given how both Houses of Congress flipping appears to be a foregone conclusion now. Everyone pinned their hopes and dreams on the honeymoon period for a new CEO's first earnings call causing AAPL to rise and stop the bleeding on SPY. Unfortunately that did not materialize as institutional traders are not happy about the increased memory costs. The bubble may be popping, and with the prospect of renewed hostilities with Iran, and paralyzed government as the economy is teetering, we may be looking at a perfect storm setting up here. So let's dive right in and see where today left us and then we can figure out what the next move may be.
First thing is first, SPY closed below the 100DMA for the second session in a row, that means we have flipped it to resistance. Not good. The 20DMA is now racing down toward the 50DMA again, and with the amount we are down, even though the spread between them is still $2.11, we are now on death cross watch. We tried to reach the 10DMA today but couldn't make it.  The rejection occurred at the 763.34 dark pool level that hit the tape on 10/23.
You'll also notice out massive dark pool level at 762.27 has been removed from the chart as it was not respected at all today. I had my suspicions yesterday that liquidity ran out there given how we broke down, and that would appear to be the case. We do however have a new dark pool level to add to the chart today for 2BLN at 755.62 which is right around yesterdays closing price. It also given the size may end up functioning as a temporary ceiling. Currently where we closed there are no dark pool levels until we get to 740.48, and the only things standing in between us and that are the 2 horizontal support levels, which are not dark pools they are based on the chart behavior so they may not be as strong or even respected at all.
We are below the lower bollinger band, but RSI is not oversold yet so while it may be pointing to an impulsive move that will eventually need a small bounce, we are not at the point in which a band to band reversion would be on the table.
On the topic of the oscillators, the RSI is widening away from the signal line as we move lower, so this very well looks like an impulsive move, but the volume behind it means this is not a trap. The stochastic entered the lower extreme today, but remember it can still go much lower, and stay down there tangling for a while if need be. The MACD gave us another red bar and the line is firmly in the negative now. The AO gave us another red bad, so it would seem Thursday's green bar was a trap after all this is why when we get a bar color change I never go off just one, I always wait to see if a new trend develops.

The 1H offers no comfort as there is still some room to the downside and the RSI has been slow dancing with the signal line drifting down all day, technically it ended in a bearish arrangement, but I always treat it as indecision when they are tangling like this. The other oscillators still have a clear bear trend. It also doesn't help that the last candle of the day was a rejection of the 754.05/754.07 double dark pool level.

In order to get a chart that give any measure of hope that the bleeding stops you have to go all the way down to the 15m. Here it is getting pretty oversold, and we did just close a whisker under the lower bollinger band, unfortunately for the bulls a band to band reversion only gets us to a retest of the 100DMA.

No MOC today, I forgot to grab it. Just know that it started off with a 1.19BLN buy side that got eaten into a minor sell side.
Let's take a look at gamma. You know what, you really don't want to look at this. You do? Well alright don't say I didn't warn you. Viewer discretion advised. First lets start with the easy part, what we don't have, which is any call level with a net > 1BLN. Now here comes the ugly part. We have put walls in excess of -1BLN at 750, 740, and as of today 730. Also you might be tempted to get hope because there is a net 711MLN at the call wall at 800, unfortunately that is counter-balanced by 700 having a net -786MLN now.

Taking a look at the heatmaps for GEX the one bright spot is based on the Volume and OI at 750 for tomorrow that came in today it would appear that that could be where we bounce, or at least try to. Though not much is really built up overhead so I wouldn't count on it lasting unless that changes tomorrow.

Bonds are in a funny place right now, there is a possible regime shift underway so their cross asset signal ability is diminished here. Just know that right now the 10 Year yield is trying to find some support at the 4.885% level. If we have shifted into bonds are a safe haven trade then this may be good for stocks. We need to wait and see on this though as the flattener we got today is what is mucking things up. I want to see the short end yields come in hard relative to the long end before I can be comfortable enough to say we have had a change in regime.

The DXY's continued resilience will provide a headwind for stocks and will continue to hurt megacap earnings moving forward. What should have been a pullback has morphed into a pullback in time as we grind sideways. The bollinger bands are still pretty tight so I would say that a large move up or down should reveal itself sooner rather than later.

WTI came in by -6.5% today, and stocks still fell. There really was no concrete, even by this market's low standards, headlines that could account for such a large move down, especially with all the sabre rattling going on. This is pure speculation on my part, but I think oil traders see the inevitability that the Democrats will secure a large enough margin in both houses tomorrow that enables them to legislate a forced end to this war. Although with Hakeem Jefferies (or as we on the Democratic committee like to call him "AIPAC Shakur") projected to be speaker I wouldn't go all in on that bet.

Gold managed a 50% retrace of yesterdays move, though I would be careful here. It is still in a positive correlation with SPY, so that means if selling continues for stocks, then gold will follow. As to how low we can go, we don't have any large support levels until 3699.96. The only way gold can stop this bleed is by retaking 4100.

That leaves us with your friend and mine, the VIX. It should be clear by now we are in a rising vol regime now. The days of spike and crush may be behind us for a little bit. It would be a safe assumption that we will see it rise again tomorrow as event risk around the election continues to pile in. AAPL's poor earnings performance will also factor in to the jitters giving more fuel to this fire. The one bright spot is we still have one day left on that 85% chance of a bounce on SPY from closing > +10% on Friday. The VIX1D is giving us a second consecutive close above the 16.08 line, so it is decision time, if we remain above this line for another few sessions then we are likely about to see the VIX crank to 30. If we can get and stay below but remain in the expansion zone then we may get a more gradual rise on the VIX. If we get that crank to 30 I don't see that happening until the back end of this week at the earliest, but more likely next week. More evidence that we are in a rising vol regime is the VIX3M/VIX spread narrowed to 1.02 today breaking below the 1.03 I had pointed out yesterday as being the level to start getting worried.

A little bonus chart today to put things in perspective for you as to the state of where things are. Here are High Yield Credit Spreads blowing out and there are very near the March high as of today. If we blow past that then we are likely looking at the beginning of a credit event. I want to see it blow past 4 before I confirm it as such though.

My take today, woof. This goose may be cooked. Given AAPL's whiff and the fact that SPY ended on the lows today, I anticipate that we will see some continuation in the morning and try to find a short term bottom at or around 750, from there given how oversold we are unless we get an exogenous shock I would favor a relief bounce to the 100DMA which would be around 759.50 tomorrow, but realistically it may only get as far as the 755.62 dark pool from today. So I plan to play this one level to level, 750 to 755.62, then see how it behaves before targeting 759.50. I do see that bounce failing and think we are ultimately headed to the 200DMA before November OpEx, which right now is around 730. The alternative case that I honestly struggle to even envision here is we get no downside continuation and move to retake the 100DMA and make a run for the 10DMA and over the next few sessions take that move to the 50DMA. Though that too I see failing, just at a much better put entry, but with the VIX doing what it's doing I cannot endorse that. The market has entered hard mode, for the new guys and gals here's something to keep in mind, just because it doesn't drop right away, doesn't mean all is well and the worst is behind us. While bottoming is an event, topping is a process, a long, tricky, trappy, process, but once the dam breaks it gets going in a hurry. Now is not the time to press longs or buy the dip. If you have that impulse then just sit in cash and wait this out.
