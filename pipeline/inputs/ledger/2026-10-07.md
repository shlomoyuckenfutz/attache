# Wednesday 10/7 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Well I guess we are going for it. Technically the gravestone doji confirmed but SPY busted the shooting star and managed to get a second close above the 776.47 Dark Pool level. The 10 Year auction went well and the Fed minutes dropped expectations from two more hikes this year to one more. Yields and the DXY relaxed as a result. So it looks like we have a date with 785 this Friday. Lets jump in and see where today left us.
I'm not going to go into much detail about the SPY chart today because it is literally exactly the same as yesterdays write up, declining volume, 20 and 50DMAs running parallel with half a buck between them, more room to run up, yada yada yada. The only differences today is that we have a new dark pool level at today's high of 779.10 (its also a penny above yesterdays closing price so this one is a push when trying to figure out it they were buys or sells.), and the RSI and Stochastic are beginning to show some weakness, but those last two can still form a divergence with price where they are now so not an immediate concern. 
 
The 1H chart also looks like we could run a little bit more too, but not before a shallow pullback probably tomorrow morning. And when I say shallow I mean shallow, based on the stochastic as well as the little rounded top we did with the SMA 10 this afternoon, I anticipate that we will see a morning pullback to the EMA 21 which has been a last line of defense support on this time frame, that would mean the pullback would be maybe just a whisker under 776, then off the the races. I know I know "but what about the SMA 10 coming in for a bear touch with the SMA 20." Well little Jimmy that's a good question, the way I see it is if the scenario I put above plays out they will just tangle which is what is usually seen at points of indecision. I'm far more concerned about the 20DMA flirting with the 50DMA on the daily than I am the SMA 10 and 20 on the hourly.
 
The MOC today was pretty okay.  While we started with a first call of 1.7BLN that got eaten up a bit, we did still end on the buy side with 1.1BLN. Sorry bears. 
 
 
While I stopped using the Gamma levels for now when looking beyond 0dte due to the volatile swings from day to day (will use it again once it becomes more reliable), I am using open interest to determine the magnet levels. From the looks of the OI for Friday 785 is the big magnet level, and it's not even close. Also when you look at the OI for tomorrow the largest level is the puts at 775, which also reinforces the shallow morning pullback before liftoff the 1H chart is suggesting.
 
The 10 Year yield made a new intraday high today, but thanks to the good auction and the FOMC Minutes we ended up closing out the day dead flat. It seems to me that we are in a place where the yields should pull back to test the 20DMA before deciding whether or not to resume grinding higher. So figure a pullback to about 5.15% should be sufficient, and while still a bit too high for the comfort of stocks, it will take a bit of pressure off of stocks while it gets there. Same goes for the DXY and a pullback to 101.
 
 
Gold held the support at 4100 but did do some technical damage today. The bear flag also technically broke down. Losing 4100 within the next 2 days will put a crimp in the plans for SPY, unless it is a divergence in which the signal would be that the trip to 785 is going to be short lived upon reaching the destination. Remember, Gold has a positive correlation with SPY.
 
Oil is realistically the only wild card (until CPI next week) that could mess up yields pulling back. WTI is maintaining the 50DMA as support and the oscillators are still looking ready to flip to bullish, it's a coiling spring. For the next two days what we want to see it 91.08 remaining as resistance if we want yields to relax. Chopping between that and 87 would be ideal, but all it will take I a single bad headline out of Trump or Iran and this coiled spring will go boing in a bad way. My point is don't hang your hat on this.
 
The VIX is doing that thing it does when you get within a week of monthly VIXperation where it pins to a level, it would seem this month that level is 15. So while the VIX was up today, we appear to be riding the 15.20 line, on the wrong side of those tracks mind you, as we are in the beach ball zone. 
The VIX1D is appearing to have made a bottom and is making a slow grind higher. The last thing you want to see is orderly movement up on the VIX1D, that means a move is building more often than not. If you are a bull you want to see the VIX1D chopping around with wide body candles all higgeldy-piggeldy in the compression zone between 6.88 and 12.35. Likewise the VIX3M/VIX spread is effectively moving sideways since the beginning of September. Remember with this spread the trend is the signal, not a day or two, uptrend means vol compression, downtrend is vol expansion. So all signs point to the VIX being another coiling spring right now.

 
 
 
Another warning sign that while may not be trouble this week, but can develop into a problem soon enough is that Stocks Above their 50DMA ticked back down today. Given that this rally up was predicated on breadth catching up after being beaten down so much, if it is turning back down now then that catch up may have been enough to pull out of oversold to resume being beaten down. IWM following suit and getting pummeled back to its 200DMA today is supporting the assertion that the SA 50DMA may be rolling back over. If this is the case then the foundation for the rally will be gone and the only thing holding us up would be the open interest at 785, which all rolls off after this Friday.
 
 

My take here, my lean has moved from neutral to favoring the bull case this week to 785. But that is the limit, all these calls for 800 and beyond are absurd amateur nonsense. All the cracks under the surface I don't expect it to stay there long once it is reached, and I do not expect Friday to close above it, otherwise dealers have to make good on those calls they sold at that level. I expect the same thing to happen as did when we made new all time highs in June and August this year, a swift and sharp correction following the major OI magnet being reached. June was 760, August was 780 (close enough), October will likely be 785. Side note, weirdly consistent that we make a new ATH every 2 months with a month of corrective price action in between. Anyway this correction from 785 I would expect to take us to between 755 and 745 before deciding if my downside is needed or if that is enough. I would assume that is entirely dependent on the macro and geopolitical backdrop at the time once we reach that point. Until then enjoy the ride to 785 and keep in mind that it ain't gonna last. Don't be a sucker here and get rugged, play the upside and the downside but be smart about it.
