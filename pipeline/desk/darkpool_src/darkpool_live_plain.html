LEVEL STATS · TOUCH / BOUNCE / BREAK

Per-level reaction stats over the baked 5m window. Click column headers to sort.

SPY↕ TYPE↕ TOUCH↕ BOUNCE↕ BREAK↕ B vs K BOUNCE %↕ BREAK %↕ AVG REACT↕ AVG FOLLOW↕ LAST TOUCH↕
Definitions (history window of baked 5m bars):
  • Touch — 5m bar high/low within $0.10 of the level.
  • Bounce — rejected at the level and moved ≥$0.50 away without a close through.
  • Break — 5m close through the level that holds 2+ subsequent bars on the through side.
  • Bounce % / Break % — share of classified bounce+break events (touches that never bounce or break are omitted from %).
  • Avg react — mean max excursion away from the level after a bounce.
  • Avg follow — mean max excursion through the level in the hour after a break holds.
  • B vs K — compact purple (bounce) vs grey (break) bar for that level’s events.

HOW TO UPDATE

Levels load from the site Dark Pool data file.
  1. Edit the levels array (SPY prices, high → low). Label them Dark Pool only.
  2. Recompute rows from convert.spy_per_spx and convert.es_minus_spx (stored with asof + public data closes).
  3. Refresh baked bars.candles (exchange 30m bars resampled to RTH 4H, ~3m; y-range fits candles, off-chart levels listed in chips) and level_stats from Desk, then push JSON. Tag levels with status current|prior and color.
