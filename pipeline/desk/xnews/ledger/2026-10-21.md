Another nice little low volume day that gave us a close right above a critical level. Unfortunately it would appear this was the culmination of the bull trap as tech earnings have pushed SPY down significantly AH. The military buildup in the Middle East along with the evacuation of all gulf US consulates is also increasing the jitters in the market. This is where the market shifts from easy to hard mode. So lets take a look at where today left us and try to piece together where we go from here.
We managed to get a close above both the 10 and 20DMAs today and held the 50DMA as support. We also closed above the 772.22/772.43 dark pool level, which could end up being a double edged sword. Volume declined again today, and the market didn't immediately turn after the VIX front month rolled off as it appears based on the volume profile that most large participants were waiting for today's earnings to pass before making a decision. Today did succeed in yanking the slope of the 20DMA back up and flattening out the 50DMA, but the 10DMA is still sloping down rather hard, so a close below tomorrow will likely be very bearish. We almost got the bullish touch on the RSI I was looking for yesterday, and the MACD kicked out another white bar, while the stochastic began cycling up. However the AO gave us another red bar that has brought us ever closer to the flip into negative territory. In other words, this looks like a confirmed bull trap. 
 
Taking a look at the 1H time frame it appears we are running out of but not necessarily completely out of gas on this move. While the MACD kicked out two white bars at the end of the day and the Stochastic begain cycling down, the RSI still is being stubborn in treating the signal line like it has cooties and refuses to give us a bearish touch so far, and the AO is green across the board. From this time frame we can also see which dark pool levels are being treated as the most important with 769.45 continuing to be respected as it was yesterday, and the 772.22 and 773.84 also proving to be respected levels. Depending on tomorrows price action I may be able to clear off the sister levels right next to each of those last two as they didn't seem to be as respected as the ones mentioned which probably means the liquidity there has been tapped out. 
 
The MOC was once again a nothingburger.
Looking at the gamma picture, first off that 773 level that we saw yesterday on the heatmap worked out well, so from now on I will be including the spot  heatmaps in addition to the gex profiles. Right now the GEX profile sans 0dte (773) is still negative gex. We have a call wall above 1BLN at 780 for net 1.2BLN, but the put wall at 760 outweighs it with -1.5BLN. Tomorrows strip out is slightly positive, but lets see if that changes after the open tomorrow with the earnings we just got. As of right now the big level tomorrow is the call wall at 775 with a tiny put wall at 767. Looking at the spot heatmaps for gamma, charm, and vanna, we can see that the big level for tomorrow as of the close today is actually 776 across all 3, with 771 coming in a close 2nd place. As of writing SPY AH is currently just below 771 but seems to be struggling to go lower. So if tomorrow follows a similar theme as today then we could possibly see 775 after a morning shakeout. 775 also had the highest volume and has the most open interest for tomorrows expiration. 
 
 
 
The VIX continued to back off today, but whats concerning is it hardly flinched with the middle east news today. It does appear to be holding the 10DMA and the breakout, but I wouldn't rule out a true back test of the wedge before really getting moving to the upside. The VIX1D still hasn't given us a pullback signal with a touch of 6.88 and for now is still stuck in the compression zone. If the VIX comes in a bit more tomorrow then we could see 6.88 touched, though with earnings doing what they are doing AH I am not quite sure the VIX1D will like it too much. Yesterday I said I would be willing to entertain the possibility of volatility continuing to compress if the VIX3M/VIX spread widened above 1.12 today, and well they aren't making this easy for me because it ended exactly at 1.12, so this is inconclusive and we need to see how tomorrow goes before making a judgement call. 
 
 
 
Gold is essentially treading water as today pretty much was just a retracement of yesterday, and the new session as of writing appears to be doing it again. Frankly until we either break 4240 or 4100 we are going to continue this piddly chop sideways and get no clear signal.
 
Same story with WTI, though I find it perplexing that it barely flinched when Iran hit Aramco infrastructure today. We are still holding above 95.64 and the 10DMA so it will continue to have a deleterious effect on the appetite for stocks. 
 
Keeping the theme going apparently (boring), the 10 Year yield is also just jogging in place, holding above 5.1%. The narrowing of those bollinger bands is starting to get be a bit uneasy. Yields appear to be expending all their bearish energy with a pullback in time by consolidating sideways, not a great position to be in with the FOMC a week from today.
 
The DXY was also boring today and same in by a smidgen. It is still holding the EMA 21 as support, and realistically it needs to lose that in order to open the door to a deeper pullback. Again, with FOMC a week from today it is running out of time. 
 
 SPX Stocks Above their 50DMA moved higher again today. Since capitulating at the end of the quarter the snap back has been incredibly strong. I wish this was a tradable asset because calling the bottom in it was probably one of my best calls of the year. Though we still aren't really in a healthy place until it gets over 70.
 
My take today, we're pretty much still in the same framework as yesterdays path. We hit 773 today which is what we were looking for, the only change really is despite the negative reaction from the META, TSLA, IBM, and TXN earnings today, there is a possibility that we could come up to touch 775 tomorrow. If we do I will be looking very closely to see if the VIX1D gives us 6.88, if it does then it's time for me to load the boat with November and December puts. If not then we have to see how things perform. 760 by the end of Friday is starting to look less realistic, though we will see after tomorrow. Another possibility I am keeping an eye on is the formation of the right shoulder here on a head and shoulders (see bonus chart below). In case you haven't figured it out yet, there isn't a clean path at this time. This business boils down to "wait and see" 90% of the time, and while I may be kicking myself for not getting into puts at 773.84 today, it's better to miss the start of a move than to be impulsive and get it wrong. Bottom line, if a head and shoulders confirms then it significantly changes my downside targets. Likewise if we manage to close above 772.22 tomorrow then the time to the start of a correction is also kicked out further again. Markets are dynamic and constantly changing from day to day, so dogma is a luxury you cannot afford if you want to be consistently profitable.
