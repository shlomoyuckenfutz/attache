Well we got that morning pullback we were looking for yesterday, though it was a bit deeper than expected. Thanks Scam Altman. We also started to see an open interest shift at 785 from tomorrows expiration out to next Friday's monthly expiration. While gamma flipped to negative today, Vanna and Charm flows are back on the menu to buoy us into monthly OpEx next week. So lets dive in and see where today left off before opining further on the forward outlook. 
Today SPY really have the dark pool complex at 771.35 - 772.43 a good test to see if it still functioned as support, and the line held. With volume running higher than both Wednesday and Tuesday we missed the 10DMA by $0.19 but I'm willing to count it as a hold of support. We got another doji candle today, but unlike the spinning top yesterday this one has a longer bottom wick which is indicative of support being found. The dance between the 20DMA and 50DMA is getting closer with the spread shrinking by 8bps today to 0.42. Other than that no change from yesterday on the moving averages. We got a new dark pool level to add to our charts today, 1.3BLN at 773.76 which is a whisker below where we closed. Combine that level with the thrust up into close and it is readily apparent that these were buys. The MACD got a white bar today, the RSI got a bearish touch, and the Stochastic got a bearish crossover from the upper extreme. 
 
While the oscillators on the daily look like their are rolling over, the 1H chart tells a different story. Since 1:30PM today the oscillators have began building bullish momentum and the stochastic pulled itself out of the lower extreme. The SMA 50 in conjunction with the 771.35 dark pool level clearly provided solid support and the 12:30/1:30 candles would have formed a Tweezer Bottom had the lower wick on the first candle been a hair shorter. This time frame is saying to me that the daily oscillators are going to turn back up for one more push. 
 
The MOC today wasn't bullish, but it also wasn't a concerning level of bearish...yet. We started out with a first call of a buy side +1.9BLN that got eaten away into the close to a sell side of -819MLN. We are seeing a trend develop of weakness following the first call, but this trend tends to be more of an early warning sign rather than an immediate issue. If we had a sell side of -2BLN or more that would be a different story.
 
 
GEX is starting to tighten up so it is back to being a somewhat effective roadmap. The call wall is still at 785 and two call walls just 100MLN less have built up at 780 and 790. We are however in negative gamma and there is the put wall at 767 to contend with (775 is all 0dte). Once the 0dte rolls off the GEX will likely be closer to neutral, though with 0dte coming on at the same time it can go either way.  The good news for the bulls is that all of that 767 put gex rolls off after tomorrow, and the strip out for tomorrows expiration has 780 as the call wall. 785 is a nonentity at this point for tomorrow. Even more good news for the bulls is that all that 785 GEX was just pushed out to next Friday today. Looking the the 10/16 strip out we can see that is the big level. The put wall for then is at 750 so keep a close eye on that. 
 
 
 
Here's where the bulls will start pounding their chest. Since next week is monthly OpEx we need to look at Vanna and Charm flows. Now as of yesterday that was going to be stacked against the bulls, but with today's little drop hedging activity picked up and now as we can see both have a huge node at 790 that will act like a magnet. Yes you read that correctly 790. Keep in mind that this month VIXperation isn't until 10/21 so we won't have the Wednesday unmooring of volatility. Given that Max Pain for monthly OpEx is 768 and the largest open interest level is 750, the likely path here is rally into Wednesday and then give some back into Friday. 
 
 
The pullback in yields on the 10 Year is progressing the way we outlined in prior write-ups. It appears that a pullback to the 20DMA is in the cards here. We closed under both the 10DMA and the EMA 8 despite the hiccup with the 30 Year Auction. As I said yesterday I think it pulls back to 5.15. 
 
The DXY caught a break too, but unfortunately not as much. Still levitating above the 10DMA. The oscillators are actively rolling over though. Yesterday I said a pullback to 101 is possible, but now I am thinking more in the neighborhood of 101.4. If we want 785 on SPY we need this to at least get under the 10DMA.
 
Gold gave us a third day in a row testing the 4100 area support and held. So it looks like it wants to try to get back to a test of 4240. Gold running back up should help SPY. The question remains, should I be extending the lines on that bear flag and consider the last 3 days an underthrow, or is it busted. We will have to wait and see on that. The oscillators do look bullish though. 
 
The VIX is getting restless and impatient. We wicked and touched both 15.20 today and the upper trend resistance of the falling wedge. However with VIXperation not until the 21st and monthly OpEx next week, it likely missed its window to spike prior to VIXperation, which coincidentally is right where it runs out of room between 15.20 and wedge resistance. The VIX1D did not get a touch of 6.88 today but it was all over the compression zone nearly making it to the center line. Most clues that vol is restless. But it is still gently grinding higher, which as I said yesterday was indicative of a coiling spring. More evidenced by the VIX3M/VIX spread which is just grinding sideways. 
 
 
 
Stocks above their 50DMA broke out of the overhead resistance today, which means the breadth catch up continues. This catch up rally likely has legs through next week. We can also see that there is a rotation going on when we look at how RSP outperformed MAGS today, meaning most of the strength we saw was from the other 493. Unfortunately it looks as if IWM is no longer a viable proxy to trade the breadth catch up as it is beginning to show weakness again on a relative basis. If it loses that 200DMA you don't want to be long small caps. 
 
 
 

So here's how I see it, 785 is off the table tomorrow, but the bull case is not busted, just delayed. As I said above my base case is we rally into Wednesday and make a run for 790, which I don't know that we make it there, but there is a good chance we reach 785 by next Wednesday. I would sell that level, HARD. We likely give back some into OpEx on Thursday and Friday. Then after VIXperation is when the rug likely get pulled. It really all depends on the geopolitical situation and Scam Altman keeping his fat mouth shut. Those are the wild cards. If that goes swimmingly then the above is my base case. Now if we lose the 10DMA tomorrow then we are probably headed for a death cross and at that point 755 becomes the level to plan for with 764.19 being the major level that is definitely going to be the magnet in that scenario. Right now this is not my lean (*insert surprised pikachu face*), and I am leaning toward bull now, bear after OpEx. Right now this move that we have been in since September 17th looks like a 3 drives to a high with the first two drives completed. 785 would be number three, and if this is a 3 drives to a high then the other side is going to be a sharp correction, so if we get the move to 785 and you keep rolling the dice for 790 you will crap out big time. I want to see the 776.47 dark pool retaken tomorrow ideally, but I must see the new dark pool at 773.76 hold as support. We get that then I am a raging bull until next Wednesday, if we don't then I start leaning more to the bear case. As always, but patient and don't force a position early, wait for confirmation always.
