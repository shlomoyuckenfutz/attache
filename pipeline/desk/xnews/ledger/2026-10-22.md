# Thursday 10/22 SPy Post-Market Write-Up

Is everyone having a good time? Boy oh boy was my lean yesterday wrong. Turns out growth scares DO make the market go down after all. We lost some critical levels today and tested the big kahuna again, so lets dive in and see where today left off.
SPY gave us a nice -1.18% day, by no means a crash or even a gut check. It goes without saying that the break above the 20DMA and 10DMA yesterday was a false breakout which does not bode well moving forward. The 50DMA is also in danger of being flipped to resistance with a close below today. We will need a close back above to hang on to the 760s. Speaking of moving averages, the 10DMA is now a whisker away from a bearish crossover with the 20DMA. The 20DMA has also turned back downward, and the 50DMA is slightly sloping down now. The EMA 8 also crossed under the EMA 21. We tested the big kahuna of dark pool levels again today, 762.27. Remember, every test, especially the ones that violate it, eats up the liquidity at that level, eventually it will dry up and that's when the ground disappears from underneath your feet. SPY attempted to retake the 764.19 level into close but couldn't make it. The oscillators did just as I have been saying the probable case is since Tuesday, the RSI ricocheted off of the signal line and turned back down, and the Stochastic is now in a bearish touch. The MACD give us a red bar and the lines are flirting with going negative. The AO give us our first red bar in the negative territory today. 
 
It's not all doom and gloom though, because when we look at the 1H chart we can see that SPY looks to want to try to stage one more attempt at recovering. The last two candles of the day appear to have found support at the massive dark pool level and are turning up. The MACD kicked out 2 white bars, the RSI is heading for a bullish touch, and the stochastic is showing signs it is ready to cycle back up from the lower extreme. The AO also gave us a green bar for the last hourly candle. 
 
The MOC wasn't encouraging starting at a buy side just shy of 3BLN and getting eaten away to a non-actionable buy side of just 145.1MLN. What I glean from this however is that we aren't about to sell off tomorrow as we didn't dip into the sell side. Make no mistake it is weak, but we aren't  running off the cliff yet.
 
 
As far as the gamma profile is concerned we are still negative gex, but the only material change today was the erasure of the call wall at 780. The put wall remains at 760 and only added a little bit to the gex there to bring it to -1.8BLN. There is a minor put wall at 765 now though. Looking at the spot heatmaps we can breath a sigh of relief for tomorrow as it would seem that 769 is in play and things don't get ugly until Monday. If we manage to move lower tomorrow it looks like 760 will provide a measure of support.
 
 
Lets talk about yields first. The 10 Year lost 5% today in a big way. Now up to this point that would have been bullish for stocks, however we may be changing regimes here. If we are in a legitimate growth scare then the negative correlation between SPY and yields will flip to positive, meaning that bonds are being used as a safe haven. We can see further evidence that this may be a flight to safety based on the fact the the long end is where most of the demand was while the short end barely moved. It is honestly too soon to tell for sure of we have changed regimes, and I am reserving judgement on that until we get through FOMC next Wednesday.
 
 
The DXY resilience in the face of bond yields sinking also point to a potential safety trade. Again, we need to keep an eye on it and confirm, it is premature to make any judgements just yet. 
 
One potential crimp in the growth scare case is what gold is doing. We lost 4100 today and are a whisker away from 4000. Losing 4000 would open the door to much lower prices. If this was a growth scare flight to safety then gold would be rising not falling. Typically.
 
However, there is a sign that the recession trade may be starting here, as utilities far and wide outperformed SPY today. This is not a good sign. While not an immediate sign of a crash, it is something to keep an eye on, because if it becomes a trend then we have a problem. 
 
This brings us to the VIX. We had a nice spike today, but it did close +14.10% which means there is now an 85% probability that SPY bounces within the next 2 trading sessions, my money is literally on tomorrow. The VIX1D came up to tap the top of the expansion range which while not as reliable as a tap of 6.88 for a pullback, this tap does give a decent chance that SPY has a bounce tomorrow. Make no mistake though, it is basing higher now which means we have only just begun to see volatility wake up. More evidence that we may see vol try to come in a bit tomorrow is although the VIX3M/VIX spread narrowed today, it couldn't take out the most recent low, which means we could still be consolidating.
 
 
 
SPX Stocks Above their 50DMA also ripped today. So while tech got clobbered today, breadth was very healthy. 
 

Here's my take: First of all, it's too soon to know for certain if this is a growth scare, so lets slow our roll here. We need more data before making any decisions based on that thesis. As for SPY tomorrow, I see us bouncing, at least to the 768.58 dark pool level which will not only give us a re test of the 50DMA, but it will also fill the gap left today. I do think that bounce will ultimately be rejected and Monday is shaping up to break down the massive dark pool level and send us to the 757.40 level. That's my current lean. If we do not bounce tomorrow I would expect us to go no lower than 760, which would set up a face ripper for Monday so long as it finds support at 760, in this case the next opportunity for SPY to drop would be after the FOMC. Be patient, stay disciplined, and wait for confirmation. This is the type of trappy price action that is designed to fool both bulls and bears.
