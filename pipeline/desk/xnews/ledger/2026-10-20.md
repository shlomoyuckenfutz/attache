# Tuesday 10/20 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

TACO attempts aside we got the continued bounce we anticipated today, but volume was much lighter and we have VIXperation tomorrow morning. While we got a wider range candle today, the price movement sans TACO looked pretty weak. Let's dive in and see where today left us.
SPY had a level respecting day, where the low bounced off the dark pool level at 764.19 and rejected off of the dark pool level at 772.22. While we did eek out a close above the 50DMA, it isn't trustworthy since it only pushed above at literally the final 5 seconds of the day. Yes you read that right, 5 seconds. So the read here is more a statistical error than a true breakout. So this will need to be confirmed tomorrow with a clean close above the 50DMA otherwise this was just shenanigans. We rejected the 20DMA which is still curling down, and couldn't quite make it to the 10DMA which is plunging down and just about in confluence with the 772.22/772.43 dark pool level. If you look closely you can also see the bollinger bands are sloping down now too. The EMA 8 and EMA 21 also have a bearish touch to end the day. While not something that triggers trading programs typically, it does signal that momentum is rolling over to the downside. That said, the oscillators are perking up. The MACD gave us a white bar today, but is is still careening toward the 0 line. The RSI ticked up again and is just a whisker under 50, I'd be willing to bet we get a bullish touch with the signal line before it gets reflected back down. The stochastic had a bull cross in the lower extreme, but the AO still kicked out a red bar and is flirting with flipping into the negative side. 
 
The 1H is not looking so hot comparatively speaking. We can see from this chart that when you strip out the TACO candles that the dark pool level at 769.45 is providing some pretty stiff resistance, much the same as it did yesterday. The MACD continues to weaken, the RSI is dancing with the signal line just below 50, and the stochastic has rolled over and is now cycling down from the upper extreme. Also of note the final hour which includes that last 5 second pump gave us a red bar, so it's calling BS on that. Additionally the bollinger bands have begun to narrow, remember they are like a coiling spring, the tighter they get the bigger the resulting move will be. 
 
The MOC today was a snooze, but still a consistent sell side. 
 
 
No GEX data today because VAULT_SRC was down, again. Based on the GEX_PANEL GEX profile for tomorrows strip out it looks like the big level is 768. When we look at the spot gamma, charm, and vanna heatmaps we can also see that same as it was yesterday, the big upside level is 773.
 
 
The 10Year yield is ostensibly jogging in place and did not really make any progress in either direction. The support that formed at 5.065% appears to be solid. Though the 10 and 20DMA did get a bearish cross today. 
 
The DXY was ever so slightly lower today, but it is still holding the EMA 21. The bollinger bands are starting to get pretty tight so we could see a big directional move soon.
 
WTI did exactly nothing today except give us a doji cross. The TACO attempt made it to the 95.64 level and bounced, meaning we have firm technical support there.
 
Gold is calling BS on the up move in SPY, diverging from the positive correlation for the second day in a down. We've seen divergences like this recently and they always end up with SPY getting yanked back in the direction gold was heading already. The oscillators also look weak here.
 
Coming to the VIX, it is plainly obvious that most of the upward bias on SPY today came from the VIX being suppressed ahead of tomorrow morning's monthly VIXperation. The big call level for this contract is 20, so the big guys keeping it under there until it rolls of should have been expected. It also means this pin is gone after the settlement tomorrow morning. The VIX1D had a pretty muted day but looks to be basing at a higher floor for a move into the expansion zone after the VIX contracts roll off. The spread on the VIX3M/VIX widened a bit today but is still well within tolerance for an expanding vol regime, if we close tomorrow above 1.12 then we can have a conversation about it maybe being different. 
 
 
 
We started tech earnings today, and it was a mixed start. While SPCX is +4.82% as of writing, it's critical to note that at current levels there will be a significant amount of stock for sale at the open along with vol crush as the implied move was 7.4% and the big call level was 155 which it seems to have trouble reaching right now. NFLX is getting slammed on earnings -5.85% as of writing this. So overall not a great start. Tomorrow on top of VIXperation we will have TSLA, META, TXN, and IBM, all stocks that can trigger a selloff in SPY if they do not deliver. 
 
 
Lastly, Stocks above their 50DMA had quite the day. For the Fibonacci enjoyers it reached the 61.8% retracement level today, so we are at the decision point. 
 
Here's my take today, first off, unless megacap tech can deliver the goods over the next 2 weeks then we are probably going to see a large tech correction as there is already evidence of a significant rotation out going on. As far as SPY goes, same lean as yesterday, a morning attempt to move up followed by weakness after the VIX contracts settle. Based on the gamma complex I would expect 772.22 to be tested one more time tomorrow, and if we end up closing back below the 50DMA then we are likely heading into correction at least to the end of the month. If we manage to hold above then I would expect chopping action with tech earnings, but no clear trend. If for some reason we manage to get a clean break out the 772.22/772.43 dark pool levels then I do not see us getting higher than 777.86 over the next few days, and as usual I expect the time above the 772 dark pools to be short lived.
