# Friday 10/18 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Well I hope everyone enjoyed this OpEx, though I suspect there are a lot on here that got today wrong, and I was one of them. All signs were pointing to a max pain pin around 770 but the ai bubble beginning to burst along with the amped up middle east tensions had other ideas and we ended the day well below 770. We are clearly at a decision point in the market now, to correct or not to correct, that is the question. Before we take a stab at the answer to that, lets take a look at where we left off today.
As pointed out in Thursday's write up, that double dark pool level at 772.43/772.22 is a critical level that SPY does not like to spend a lot of time above and when it rejects it typically sends it much lower. Well we got just that today. We tore through many dark pool levels and made it all the way to the largest on the map since August 4th, 762.27. Despite breaking below for part of the day the liquidity there provided a measure of support. This is a loaded level, so it will take a lot for it to go, but once it goes it will GO quickly. Volume was much higher today but some of that also may be accounting for OpEx hedge unwinds. We have not confirmed the loss of the 10DMA, and we are working on confirmation of the loss of the 20DMA and the 50DMA. The 10DMA has also begun to slope down toward the 20DMA, and the 20DMA has flattned out while the 50 DMA remains flat. If price stays below the 50DMA then by mid to late week we should see a death cross of all three. we are also now only 0.74% above the 100DMA which puts it in play. The oscillators are now in a full on bearish arrangement and have plenty of room to run. The AO nailed the turn on Tuesday while the other oscillators faffed about. 
 
We're in a bit of trouble when we look at the weekly time frame, as it would appear that we are riding the lower support of a large rising wedge. When this breaks then we are going to likely start a bear market type correction. 
 
It's not all doom and gloom, as the 1H looks like it wants to stage a mechanical bounce here off of the 762.27 dark pool. In addition to the clear basing at this darkpool going on here, we can also wee that the 1:30 candle closed below the lower bollinger band while the RSI was below 30. This was also replicated on the 30m time frame as well. As we know this is a mean reversion signal that is pretty reliable in sending price back to the opposite bollinger band before rolling back over. A dead cat bounce. Judging where the upper BB is on the 1H by Monday it should reach confluence with the 772.43/772.22 double dark pool level, that is where I would expect one last test before we give up the ghost. 
 
The MOC is also supportive of a bounce here. While it did lose a bit of steam from the first call of a buy side 2.418BLN to a last call of 1.148BLN it still maintained a buy side above 1 billion. This also speaks the the amount of liquidity present at 762.27.
 
 
Looking at GEX across all expiries and for the time being although it is put dominated, all the levels exceeding -1BLN net GEX are clustered locally, but critically there are no call levels exceeding 1BLN. 
 
But when we look on the gamma heat map we see this support may be short lived. 769 is the stand out level for Friday of this upcoming week, but then when we look out to the end of the month there is a very large amount of gamma piled up down at 750.
 
For the sake of brevity I am going to combine the DXY, 10 Year Yield, and Gold in this one section. Starting with gold, while it did close above the 10DMA it rejected the 20DMA and it is running out of time on the stochastic. Same story but reversed with the DXY, it lost the 10DMA but held the 20DMA and is also running out of time on the stochastic. The 10 Year yield has lost both of those MAs and the stochastic has run out of room, though I would be careful here, as we may be looking at a regime change where treasuries are the safety trade over stocks, we will need to watch closely this week to confirm. 
 
You're all waiting for the real story though, the VIX. Yes we have a breakout of the falling wedge finally, but it should come in Monday to re test the 200DMA. Since it closed +21.46% there is now a 95% probability that we have a bounce on SPY within the next 2 trading sessions. The VIX1D supports this as it went much too far our of the range which generally causes some mean reversion before resuming the vol spike, at least on initial breaks like this one. The VIX3M/VIX spread narrowed considerable and as such is confirming that we are now at the beginning of a period of elevated volatility. Also to keep in mind is that we have a minor backwardation within the VIX term structure. Nothing catastrophic yet as it is only the front 2 months, but should this continue or get worse after VIXperation on Wednesday then we will have a real problem on our hands like March.
 
 
 
 
Lastly, believe it or not, but stocks above their 50DMA actually ticked up today. Which means that this correction will be slamming tech most likely.
 

My take here is that it feels and in many ways looks an awful lot like the end of February right now. I think we put in the high for a while and won't be making a new one, at least in the next few weeks. My base case is that we get a bounce early this week to 768 with 772 as the stretch, ultimately we will reject and end the month at or near 745. Why 745 rather than 750 like the gamma node? Because 745 puts have had many many many whale hits for about two months now so it seems the big guys really want to see that number on their screens, so that is where we are likely headed. We need to break down 72.27 and then the double level at 757.43/757.40 first. The alternative path here which is not my lean is we recover 772.43 and re-establish it as support, then I see us getting at least to 777.86, but even in this more bullish case I do not see a new ath, there is just too many jitters, and with the midterms rapidly approaching and early voting started in some states, those market jitters are going to continue to ramp up to closer we get. Let's see how things look after VIXperation on Wednesday, as the first two days of this week should be a trap. Stay vigilant and don't get greedy or emotional here.
