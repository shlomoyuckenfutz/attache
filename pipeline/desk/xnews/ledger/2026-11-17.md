Today will not be nearly as long as yesterday as we are still well within the expected path, and aside from SPY reaching the 200DMA ahead of schedule, not much changed across other assets that wasn't expected. So lets dive right in and see where the charts left off today. 
After spending most of the afternoon below it, SPY closed right on the 200DMA today with what I like to call the 'Humpty Dumpty Candle' since  it's sitting on the wall (200DMA) and has its leg dangling below. Much like Humpty Dumpty this setup threatens a great fall, but more on that later in my take. Aside from the interaction with the 200DMA the other moving averages are on the same path as yesterday and the spread between the 50DMA and 100DMA is now 2.78 which is a whole 0.70 narrower than yesterdays close. So this is the real threat here, if these death cross it will trigger another leg down like the 20/50 death cross did. Volume was running higher today than it was yesterday. We did get two new dark pool levels today, 1.3BLN at 732.14 and 1.4BLN at 739.73. That last one puts it right next to yesterdays new level at 739.72 for 2.3BLN. Let's not fool ourselves though, below today's close we still have a scant amount of dark pool liquidity to hold us up, so we need a rally and fast if we are going to have a stay of execution. The oscillators are also much the same as yesterday with all four universally moving down in a bearish arrangement. 
 
The 1H chart shows us how much heavy lifting that last line of defense dark pool at 729.26 today. I'm actually kind of surprised it held so well given this was from July. We closed the day with the MACD heading for a bullish touch, the RSI at a bullish touch after spending most of the day oversold, the stochastic is already in a bullish cross trying to cycle up out of the lower extreme, and the AO gave us a green bar to end the day. Of particular interest to me is the 10:30am candle closed below the lower bollinger band while the RSI was oversold, which is a mean reversion setup for a band to band move. Considering the upper band is at 758.48, even half of that distance would be a substantial and violent bounce. Like all signals it is not a guarantee nor is it fool proof. 
 
The MOC which started at a decent sell side imbalance ended with a final call of buy side 1.286BLN.
 
 
Gamma is still a nightmare. While we saw some call buildup at 775 to make it the new call wall, the net gamma there is still only 597MLN. Meanwhile the put walls are multiplying like rabbits. The main put wall is now at 730, closely followed by 725, and in 3rd place we now have -1.1BLN at 700 (uhoh), and we also have walls on the verge of crossing the -1BLN threshold at 720 and 710. The big put level for Friday's expiration is still 725, with no calls in sight. 
 
 
The DXY is still diverging with bond yields and gold, however there is one hiccup today that prevents us from saying there is a flight to safety trend forming here and that's where on the curve the buying was. They were buying the long end more than the short end today so it muddys the waters here. 
 
The VIX spent another day up more than 10% which puts another 85% probability of an up session for SPY within one of the next two sessions. Combined with the fact that yesterdays did not hit today that places a very good chance tomorrow sees a vol crush rally. This is bolstered by the VIX1D closing well above the 23.16 line which is a line it does not like to close consecutive sessions above, even during a vol spike (occasionally it will but that's when you have a 30+ VIX). Tomorrow will be important to give us direction for the VIX moving forward as we will get to see if it maintains 16.08 as a floor or cracks back into the normal range. If we maintain 16.08 then we are in for a much larger spike. I suppose it's poetic that this also comes with VIXperation being tomorrow morning. The VIX3M/VIX spread continues to narrow, though not to the extent of yesterday. 
 
 
 

So here's my take. We are ripe for a bounce, and since we actually touched the 200DMA today and NVDA didn't eat it after earnings I think tomorrow is the day. Given that we got a 2.3BLN dark pool at 739.72 yesterday and then another 1.3BLN at 739.73 today I think this is where we are heading tomorrow. It also gets us back above the 733 and 738 clusters. How long we last up there depends on how much liquidity is left in those clusters after today. My stretch would be a re-test of the 10DMA within the next 2 sessions. The fact that we did technical damage to the 200DMA today and that we completed a Three Black Crows candle pattern leads me to believe this will be nothing more than another lower high being developed that will roll back over, potentially by Thursday as there is still a lot of gamma, vanna, and charm at 725 for Friday's monthly expiration. So at the first hint of trouble tomorrow I will be selling my calls to play it safe. The alternative path is asia demolishes this after hours rally action and we break the 200DMA. I give this slim odds though. Just remember, so not trust the rally unless we break last Thursday's high.
