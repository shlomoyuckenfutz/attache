# Friday 10/23 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

Today SPY asked us "what's another half a percent among friends?" My lean from yesterdays write-up was wrong, however the secondary case was dead on accurate with 760 being the pin. With how this week ended the bulls need to pray for a miracle next week, otherwise November is going to be an ugly month. So lets see where today and this week left us.
SPY gave us a doji today closing three whole pennies below the 760 gamma pin. However if you squint closely enough you can see that the candle body is hollow red, which on hollow candles can indicate a potential bottom, like everything it is not gospel. The big story however is that we closed below the 762.27 dark pool level, which is a very very bad sign. We did however get a new dark pool level today to add to the chart, 1.2BLN at 763.34, unfortunately that was at this morning's high, so there's a good chance they were sells. Taking a look at the moving averages the picture is not looking great. We got a bear cross of the 10 and 20 DMAs today and the 10 is plunging toward the 50DMA. Meanwhile the 20DMA is sloping down at the same angle as the 50DMA again, so we would need more selling to produce a death cross. We came within spitting distance of the 100DMA today, but couldn't quite make it. On the oscillators we are back in bearish alignment across the board, the MACD not only kicked out a red bar but the MACD line went into negative territory today. The RSI continues to plunge, however its divergence from the signal line which although sloping down as well is doing so in a more gradual way, is suggesting that this move may be impulsive. We most recently saw the RSI do this last Friday and ended up snapping back on Monday. The Stochastic continues to cycle down back toward the lower extreme, and the AO gave us a second red bar in the negative territory. 
 
When we look at the weekly chart things look much more dire as we cleanly rejected the 10 Week MA, and the low was right on the 20 Week MA. The MACD kicked out another negative red bar, the RSI is pushing down harder, and the Stochastic is cycling down toward the lower extreme. The AO gave us another red bar, and has been nothing but red bars since September 8th. Why does it matter since we held the 20 Week MA you ask? Well Jimmy, here's the deal, if we look at the monthly candle in progress that will complete next week as it stands right now it is a clear top candle and the fact that it is a gravestone doji implies it is also a reversal candle. The monthly oscillators are all starting to roll over too. So unless we can print a healthy rally next week then we may be looking at our goose being cooked next month, actually more so our Turkey being cooked since it is November. 
 
 
It's not all hopeless, if we look on the 1H chart it actually looks even more ripe for a snapback than yesterdays close did. The Stochastic is cycling back up now, the MACD was consistently white bars all day, and the RSI ended with a bullish touch. 
 
The MOC believe it or not bucked the trend by starting with a first call of sell side -1.256BLN that was bought up into the close to settle out as a meager sell side of -202.2MLN. 
 
 
The gamma profile is where things start to look less hopeful. We have a new put wall and it is at 750 for a net of -2.6BLN. 760 will be -1.7BLN once 0dte rolls off, and 761 is entirely 0dte. We have another put wall just a whisker less than 750 at 755. Critically also we have no call walls over 1BLN. 
 
When I look at the spot heatmaps for Gamma, Vanna, and Charm, the first thing that sticks out at me is all three have a massive node for next Friday at 755, and when we compare it to the new put walls on the profile, it is a pretty good bet that we are ending next week around 755 if something doesn't change soon. There is a glimmer of hope for Monday though if you look at the volume for the GEX heatmap and we can see that 765 got a lot of bullish call action today.
 
 
The VIX moved up again today, but it is starting to get a bit more methodical and less impulsive about it. Two important takeaways from the VIX today; first it is clearly having difficulty establishing above 20, and second we still have that 85% probability of a bounce in SPY for Monday based on it rising > 10% yesterday and we didn't have a bounce today. More evidence we may see a bounce Monday is the VIX1D closed above the 16.08 line, so unless we are in the opening inning of a crisis spike in the VIX, it doesn't like to stay above there more than a session or two, so the resulting vol crush will buoy stocks. This is further supported by the VVIX which put in what looks like a topping candle today, and the spread on the VIX3M/VIX only narrowing today by 0.01. If we were in the beginning of a large vol spike then it would have taken out last Friday's low. 
 
 
 
 
Moving on to Gold, it is trying to find support around 4000, and we are right at where late June and July found a bottom. This was also a bottoming level for last October. The risk here is if this support breaks then there is nothing to stop price from drilling until 3700.
 
The primary driver for SPY moving down again today was Oil. WTI is now back near the September high. However when you look at Brent things are much worse are it broke the September high and is now flirting with the top from the beginning of the crisis. Keep in mind, Brent is what is mainly used for winter mix gasoline refining. 
 
 
The 10 Year yield missed re taking 5% today by 0.001%. With FOMC next week we are likely going to see a decision made on whether or not the regime has changed, but as of today we are back with the negative correlation as usual. Same with the DXY which is just bumping along the EMA 21 right now.
 
 

So my take here is this, Monday looks ripe for a bounce to 765. From there I would expect Tuesday to be flat given FOMC is the following day. After that it's up to the Fed, if Warsh is hawkish and/or hikes then 745 to 750 is likely where we end the week. Dovish Warsh would not necessarily make us rip to new highs, but it would be a stay of execution and allow us to continue in this chop range. If we close below 760 next week then we are most likely staring down the barrel of a full on 5 - 10% correction following the midterms. The alternative case for next week is Monday and Tuesday keep selling until we reach the dark pool support at 757.43 and the Fed helps us bounce. Either way any stay of execution will likely not lead to another new high this year. The choices I see before us are chop or correct.
