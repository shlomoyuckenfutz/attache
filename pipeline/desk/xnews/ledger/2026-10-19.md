Houston, we may have a problem. Was SPY up today, yes. Was it good enough, no. Although for a while it looked like we may have had a stay of execution but the end of the day made sure that was not the case. Right now all that is holding SPY together is that we have VIXperation in 2 days and the big guys are attempting to keep the VIX below 20 until then. Let's take a look at where today leaves us before deciding whether or not we are screwed and by how much.
Today printed an inside day, that's the good news as this is a bullish harami. The bad news is we lost the 50DMA and the 20DMA in the same day.  Both are now confirmed to be broken down. SPY only made it just past my initial bounce target from Friday, but we couldn't reach the 772.22 dark pool level. The 10, 20, and 50DMAs are all now sloping down, and the more time we spend under the 50 the more extreme the slope on the 20 will become until we produce a death cross. The double dark pool level at 767.41/767.42 (I really need to rework how these are charted) was the clear resistance level today. When we look at the oscillators we can see the MACD is still rolling over, and the AO printed another red bar, but the Stochastic and the RSI look like they want to make an attempt at a bullish touch. 
 
The 1H doesn't look great, but there is still some room for a push up on the oscillators, and holding the SMA 10 supports just that. 
 
The MOC today highlighted the weakness in the system. While we ended with a final call at a buy side of 447MLN, the first call of sell side -3.61BLN is a shot across the bow. Institutions are showing an appetite for unloading risk here. 
 
 
Gamma isn't a pretty picture after today. Remember in Friday's write-up how I pointed out the put gex was clustered locally and there was only at the money levels exceeding 1BLN? Yeah, that's gone. The largest put wall has now rolled down to 750 for -1.6BLN (767 is all 0dte). 
 
Looking at the spot gex heatmap we can see that the implied path here is, 771 tomorrow, 773 Wednesday likely at the open before the VIX front month  rolls off. Then it id down to 762 which happens to be where that massive dark pool level that couldn't be broken Friday is lurking. Don't take it as gospel as it can easily change tomorrow, but for now this is the path it is implying. 
 
The VIX came in a bit today, the big level for the front month is 20, so the big guys are going to do their best to keep it under that until the roll off Wednesday morning. Though it is not particularly comforting to see the doji cross it printed showing much more upward bias than downward. The VIX1D also backed off, and it is bumping at the underside of the center line today. If you made me guess right now then I could see us touch 6.88 tomorrow. If we do then grab a helmet. 
 
 
Unfortunately vol was the only thing confirming today's rally attempt in SPY. Everything else disagrees. Starting with Gold, we have a divergence today with it losing the 10DMA and ending down. 4100 is still the line in the sand here, if that breaks then 4000 comes quick.
 
Despite having a down day, WTI is still uncomfortably high and showed quite a bit of resilience. We are still above 95.64 which means it will be a headwind for stocks. 
 
The DXY found support with the 20DMA and bounced off it today after its second test. If it gets back above the 10DMA it will resume its climb. The RSI has now pulled back enough to allow for another leg higher and the stochastic looks like it wants to cycle back up. The MACD is the only oscillator that disagrees right now. 
 
Same story with the 10 Year yield, except here the MACD agrees and printed a white bar. Based on the last three sessions it would appear that 5.065% is a pretty solid support level and we are now bouncing. Given what oil and the DXY are doing and the bellicose language around Iran continuing, there is a high probability we are starting the next leg up and that last week was not a flight to safety but rather just a technical pullback in yields. 
 
Stocks above their 50DMA checked back a whisker today, but nothing to be concerned about just yet. If we decline again tomorrow then we may have a problem. 
 

My take today is it doesn't look good, but the fat lady hasn't sung yet either. I think we get a slow grinding bounce attempt continuing to at latest Wednesday morning. Gamma seems to agree with this assessment. At the very least I can see us trying to reclaim the 50dma tomorrow. It likely fails. While it is still possible we could retest 772.22, the chances are getting slim, we are just running out of time and there is too much dark pool and gamma resistance in the way. After VIXperation I can see us ending the week in the neighborhood of 760, but realistically if we manage to get there it will mean we broke down the massive dark pool level at 762.27 at which point the next stop is 757.40. After that we have an air pocket all the way to 746. If by some chance we reclaim the 50DMA and 772.43 before Wednesday then we have a chance at keeping this afloat a bit longer, but I do not think it would mean a new all time high is incoming, more so a sideways chop, so basically continuing what we've done for months. This isn't my lean right now, I see a correction kicking off here so while I do see the potential for another attempt to push up, I am not inclined to pick up those pennies in front of the steam roller. I am going to wait to see where tomorrow takes us, and if there is any sort of gap up and/or rejection of the 20DMA then I am opening a medium sized November put position. Best bet tomorrow is to do nothing but watch and see which setup wins the day.
