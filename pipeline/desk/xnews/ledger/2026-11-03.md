# Tuesday 11/3 SPY Post-Market Write-Up

If this was the relief bounce the VIX promised then we are in a heap of trouble. Turnout figures from early voting alone were off the charts, and when I went to vote this evening the Election Day turnout was already through the roof. This type of turnout typically benefits Democrats, which means we are likely looking at an unprecedented wave election tonight. The market will not like that for the simple fact that for the two months between now and when the new congress is seated, the President is going to go buck wild. One bright spot is AMD and SMCI earnings are reacting upward, AMD is currently beating the implied move, SMCI is not. So lets take a look at the charts and see where we closed out the day.
In yesterdays write-up I said that I saw SPY catching a relief bounce to the 755.62 dark pool primarily, and that is the level we rejected at today. We managed to close a whisker above the double dark pool level at 754.05/754.07, which based on the intraday price action appears to be our current support level. If we lose that then there is no support until 750 and then 746.77, but none of these are dark pool and just horizontal support levels based on past price action. We are still below the 100DMA and the 10DMA is nosediving toward it. Yesterday I put SPY on Death Cross watch while the spread between the 20DMA and 50DMA was 2.11, well today the 20DMA plunged to a spread of 1.05. Unless we can rocket up to 765 in the next day or two then we are likely looking at a death cross this week. If we get that then it will automatically trigger algorithms to sell and give us the next leg down. 
The oscillators are a mixed bag here, the MACD gave us another red bar and continues to move lower, and the AO kicked out a much deeper negative red bar. However the Stochastic has reached the lower extreme, and the RSI has begun to turn up back toward the signal line. The question is are we going for another bullish touch that fails, or is this time different. 
 
The 1H time frame is also a mixed bag. While we are bumping along that double dark pool level as support, at a certain point that liquidity will run out and like other massive level it will lead to an air pocket once it's drained. It's also not encouraging that the entire moving average complex on this time frame is in a bearish alignment. The MACD is kicking out green bars and trying to pull up, as is the AO. The RSI however just got a bearish crossover and the stochastic is cycling back down now. Basically the exact reverse of the daily. 
 
The MOC today went from a minor sell side to a just barely actionable buy side imbalance. 
 
 
The gamma profile remains almost exactly the same as yesterday, with no call walls, and the put walls at 750, 740, and 730. The only difference is the emergence of 745 as a put wall, and the main wall rolling down from 750 to 740. 
 
The heatmaps are implying that if we hit 755 tomorrow it will trigger a sell, but when we look at the volume we can see there was quite a bit of play at 760 for tomorrow, though it is offset by the play had at 740. Essentially implying we remain pinned in the middle, or 755 for those of you keeping score. 
 
 
My bearish tilt/lack of confidence in a recovery stems from the open interest though. You'll notices there is one number that repeatedly shows up in large quantities, 740. While it is unlikely that oi node hits tomorrow (but not out of the question), its more likely the monthly opex oi acts as the magnet to that level, and instead we see movement toward 750 for the balance of the week. You'll notice the gradual stepping down from week to week, this Friday 750, next Friday 745, and OpEx 740. Doesn't mean we perfectly follow that path, but it is the blueprint that institutional traders are laying out. 
 
Most of the following were block orders and spreads being opened, but it still isn't encouraging to see the deluge of bearish SPXW orders come over after 4pm, especially considering the bulk of them came over at the exact same time AMD earnings came out. Smells like sell the rip. 
 
Looking at the 10 Year yield which attempted to break back out above 5% and failed, it is clear that the regime has shifted and that we are now in a positive correlation between yields and SPY. How long this lasts depends on if the story of why yields are moving changes again. For now the correlation has flipped from negative to positive. 
 
The DXY remains stubbornly flat and is maintaining it's current pullback in time, which is nearing an end if the oscillators have anything to say about it. Save haven trade or run it how trade, it doesn't matter what regime we are in, a higher DXY means negative FX impacts to megacap earnings. 
 
WTI found support at 95.64, so despire the implosion on absolutely no concrete news yesterday, we are still above the level that makes oil a significant headwind for stocks. It is also a level in which energy prices start to stoke the fire under inflation. CPI and PPI this month should be fun.
 
Gold appears to be jogging in place to wait to see the outcome of the election. If the oscillators have any say in the matter there may be some bullish momentum building under the surface, but I wouldn't get too excited about that as it is being primarily driven by outside factors. 
 
SPX Stocks above their 50DMA has completely erased the implosion it had from August through September. Unfortunately that means we are now in a place where they can sell off again and we don't have breadth catchup to offset any weakness in the Mag 7. 
 
The VIX is holding above 20, which means movements in SPY price will be amplified. The VIX1D came up and closed just a whisker above the "Uh-oh Line" at 23.16. However I would attribute most of this to the election today. Theoretically we should get some measure of vol crush tomorrow as event risk is taken out of the market. That's going to be where we find out for sure if we are in a higher for longer volatility regime. If it manages to hold above the 12.35 line then 20.82 on the VIX is a bargain. Another reason I think we get some measure of vol crush tomorrow is the VIX3M/VIX spread stayed dead flat at 1.02. If the VIX1D movement had been characteristic of a wider volatility spike then we would have seen the spread narrow more. Doesn't mean we are out of the woods on vol by any means, but it does mean caution is warranted here and we need to watch what the vol complex does for the next day or two like a hawk. 
 
 
 

My take is the same as yesterday. My base case is we are in a larger move that will take us to a test of the 200DMA by or before November OpEx. In terms of tomorrow, if AMD can hold on to the gains and survive the KOSPI then we may see an attempt to push up to 760, but I thing it will be very difficult to stray too far above 755. My lean here is we close tomorrow at or below 755 producing a bearish touch of the 20/50DMAs. The alternative path here is we manage to establish above the 757.43 dark pool level which puts 760 back on the table, at which point a back test of the 100DMA is in the cards. It's less than 0.70% above so it is not entirely impossible, but I do think it is very difficult given the state of this tape which is why my lean is not this case. The next big data day will be Friday with NFP so we could see continued weakness into then, and it also means any reprieve on the volatility will be short lived as the new event risk is priced into the market. Mind you all of this is not even taking into account what happens or doesn't happen with Iran after the election. The rhythm and feel of this type of tape is very different than what you all are used to with the bull market. The days of easy low risk money are behind us, now you have to work for it. Patience, vigilance, and restraint will all be rewarded. Ignoring risk management and degeneracy will be punished severely.
